# Kauce wa Zuwa Gaggawa

Kauce wa zuwa gaggawa yana ƙidaya ziyarar sashen gaggawa (ED/A&E) da shigar asibiti na gaggawa da aka hana ta hanyar shiga tsakani na farko — manhajar rarrabewa, sa ido daga nesa, ɗakunan marasa lafiya na zahiri, karkatar da kulawa ta gaggawa. Yana mayar da "mun kama shi da wuri" zuwa da'awa mai farashi.

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

Kulawar gaggawa ita ce wuri mafi tsada na yau da kullum a tsarin (kuɗin naúrar zuwa ED a kewayen £250–£400 bisa National Cost Collection / alkaluman PSSRU; shigar gaggawa dubbai ne), kuma cunkoson ED yana haifar da jinkirin motocin ɗaukar marasa lafiya da sokewar tiyatar zaɓi. Duk abin da ke warware buƙata a farko lafiya — shawarar kula da kai, kulawar farko a ranar, amsar al'umma — yana sayo wa tsarin ƙarfi a mafi tsananin wurinsa. Wannan shi ne daidaitaccen layin fa'ida na masu duba alamomi, hidimar rarrabewa irin ta 111, da [sa ido na nesa kan marasa lafiya](../tattalin-arzikin-sa-ido-na-nesa-kan-marasa-lafiya/).

## Lissafin

```
Avoided attendances = population × (baseline rate − intervention rate)
Gross saving        = avoided attendances × unit cost per attendance
                      (+ avoided admissions × admission cost, counted separately)

Net saving          = gross saving − intervention cost − cost of new pathway usage
                      (redirected demand isn't free: a 111 call, a GP slot,
                       a virtual-ward day all have unit costs)
```

Da'awar sababi tana buƙatar abin kwatantawa: adadin zuwa yana canzawa kuma yana bambanta bisa lokutan shekara, don haka kafin/bayan kaɗai ba ya tabbatar da komai.

## Misali da aka lissafa

Hidimar sa ido daga nesa ta COPD ga marasa lafiya 3,000 masu haɗari mai yawa. Kimantawa da ƙungiyar kwatantawa da aka daidaita ta nuna zuwa ED mai alaƙa da tsananin cuta yana raguwa daga 0.9 zuwa 0.7 ga kowane majiyyaci-shekara, da shigar gaggawa daga 0.5 zuwa 0.42.

```
Avoided attendances = 3,000 × 0.2  = 600 × £300   = £180,000
Avoided admissions  = 3,000 × 0.08 = 240 × £3,800 = £912,000
Gross                                              £1,092,000/year

Costs: monitoring service £600,000; extra community-nurse responses £150,000
Net ≈ +£342,000/year — plus the QALY gains of exacerbations treated earlier.
```

A lura layin shigar asibiti shi ne ke mamaye: kauce wa zuwa kaɗai da wuya ya biya hidimar sa ido; kauce wa *shigar asibiti* shi ne inda kuɗin yake.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Wannan **tattalin arzikin kauce wa abubuwan da suka faru** ne. Darajar sa ido kan tsarin, aikawa na canary, da tsarin faɗakarwa na farko ita ce "zuwa gaggawa" da aka kauce wa — kiran gaggawa, ɗakunan yaƙi, sev-1 — kowanne da kuɗi da aka ɗora (sa'o'in injiniya × ƙima + tasirin abokin ciniki). Ƙa'idodin samfurin iri ɗaya suna aiki: a cire kuɗin sabuwar hanya ta farko (rarraba faɗakarwa ba kyauta ba ne), a yi hankali da musanyawa (faɗakarwar da ke ƙirƙirar aiki ba tare da hana abubuwan da suka faru ba damuwar lafiya ce, ba lafiya ba), kuma a tabbatar da abin da zai faru in ba haka ba da ƙungiyar kwatantawa (adadin abubuwan da suka faru na ƙungiyoyi yana canzawa kuma yana komawa tsakiya, daidai kamar zuwa ED).

## Tarkuna

- **Komawa tsakiya**: ƙungiyoyin masu haɗari da aka zaɓa a shekara mara kyau suna inganta ba tare da magani ba; ƙungiyoyin kwatantawa da aka daidaita ko ƙirar stepped-wedge suna da mahimmanci.
- **Buƙatar da samarwa ta jawo**: sauƙin rarrabewa na dijital na iya *ƙara* jimlar hulɗa (ƙarancin iyakar neman taimako) yayin rage kason ED — a ƙidaya jimlar kuɗin tsarin.
- **Ƙimanta zuwa a matsakaicin kuɗi** alhali kuɗaɗen tsayayyu na ED ba sa raguwa — duba [kuɗin ƙari da matsakaicin kuɗi](../kuɗin-ƙari-da-matsakaicin-kuɗi/).
