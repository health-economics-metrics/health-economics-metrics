# Binciken Sakamakon Kuɗi (CCA)

CCA tana gabatar da kuɗi tare da **tebur mai rarrabe na dukan sakamako** — na asibiti, na aiki, na ƙwarewa — ba tare da matsa su zuwa rabo ɗaya ko maki ɗaya ba. Mai yanke shawara yana auna ciniki a fili.

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

CCA ita ce **tsarin tattalin arziki da NICE ta fi so ga yawancin fasahar lafiyar dijital** a ƙarƙashin Evidence Standards Framework. Samfuran dijital suna haifar da tasirin da ba iri ɗaya ba (lokacin da aka ceta, gamsuwa, rage DNA, ƙananan ribar asibiti) waɗanda ke ƙin haɗawa da gaskiya zuwa lambar QALY ɗaya. Maimakon tilasta haɗakar da ba ta da ƙarfi, CCA tana nuna cikakken littafin lissafi. Ga yawancin shari'o'in kasuwanci na manhaja, ita ce mafi gaskiya kuma mafi lallashi, domin kowane mai ruwa da tsaki zai iya samun nasa jeren da ya shafi shawara.

## Lissafin

Da gangan babu dabarar haɗawa. Fitarwar tebur ne:

```
                          Intervention   Comparator   Difference
Costs (annual)            £X             £Y           ΔC
Outcome 1 (natural units) …              …            Δ1
Outcome 2                 …              …            Δ2
Qualitative outcomes      described, not scored
```

Kowane jere yana riƙe naúrorinsa. Ƙa'idoji: kowane sakamako an tsara shi tukuna (babu zaɓen cherry bayan sakamako); [mahanga](../mahangar-bincike/) da [tsawon lokaci](../tsawon-lokacin-bincike/) iri ɗaya a ko'ina; rashin tabbas ga kowane jere.

## Misali da aka lissafa

Dandamalin tantance kafin tiyata na dijital da tsarin waya, a kowace shekara, asusun asibiti ɗaya:

```
                              Digital      Phone       Difference
Running cost                  £180,000     £95,000     +£85,000
Nurse hours on assessments    6,200        11,800      −5,600 hrs
On-day surgery cancellations  92           174         −82
Patient satisfaction (CSAT)   4.5/5        3.9/5       +0.6
Assessments lost/incomplete   1.2%         4.8%        −3.6 pp
```

Babu maki ɗaya — amma shawarar tana da sauƙin tunani a kanta: £85,000 yana sayen sa'o'in ma'aikaciyar jinya 5,600 (≈ £15/sa'a, ƙasa da kowane kuɗin ma'aikata), sokewa 82 da aka kauce wa (kowanne yana ɓata zaman ɗakin tiyata na ~£1,200), da ingantacciyar ƙwarewa. Kwamiti kuma zai iya ganin daidai abin da *ba* ya samu: babu tasirin QALY ko mace-mace da aka yi da'awa.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

CCA ita ce sigar hukuma ta katin maki mai daidaito da kyakkyawar shawarar dandamali ta riga ta yi amfani da shi: kuɗi kusa da ma'aunan DORA, maki DevEx, adadin abubuwan da suka faru — ba tare da haɗawa ba. Horon tattalin arzikin lafiya da ya kamata a ƙara: **a tsara jere tukuna** (a yanke abin da ke ƙidayuwa kafin gwaji, don kada ka bar ma'aunin da ya yi muni a shiru), da **a nuna jerin marasa kyau** — CCA mai labari mai daɗi kawai tallace-tallace ne. A yi amfani da CCA lokacin da babu haɗakar da za a iya kare, wanda ga kayan masu haɓakawa kusan koyaushe haka ne.

## Tarkuna

- **Sakamakon da aka zaɓa daban** — amincin tsarin yana dogara ne a kan tsara tukuna.
- **Haɗawa ta ɓoye**: launi ko "maki gabaɗaya" suna sake gabatar da nauyin da ba na gaskiya ba da CCA ke wanzuwa don kauce wa.
- **Gurgunta shawara**: CCA tana buƙatar mai yanke shawara mai son auna ciniki; a haɗa ta da shawara da dalili.
