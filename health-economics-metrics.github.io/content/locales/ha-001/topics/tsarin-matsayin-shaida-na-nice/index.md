# Tsarin Matsayin Shaida na NICE (ESF)

ESF tsarin NICE ne da ke bayyana **yawan shaidar da fasahar lafiya ta dijital ke buƙata, daidai da haɗarinta**. Shi ne abin da ya fi kusa da amsar hukuma ga "me ya kamata mu tabbatar kafin NHS ta sayi manhajarmu?"

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

ESF (da aka fara fitarwa a 2019, aka sabunta a 2022 don ya haɗa AI da algorithms masu daidaitawa) yana rarraba fasahohin lafiya na dijital zuwa matakai bisa aikin asibiti, tare da ƙa'idodin shaida **masu tarawa** — ƙa'idoji 21 a rukuni 5 (abubuwan ƙira, daraja, aiki/tasiri, tasirin tattalin arziki, turawa):

```
Tier A — system services, no direct patient outcome (e.g., e-rostering)
         → basic standards: credibility, data protection, technical assurance
Tier B — inform, simple monitoring, communication (e.g., symptom diary)
         → + evidence of user benefit, appropriate reliability
Tier C — treat, diagnose, or actively guide clinical management
         → + high-quality comparative effectiveness evidence (ideally RCT)
           and economic analysis
```

Ga shaidar tattalin arziki, [binciken sakamakon kuɗi](../binciken-sakamakon-kuɗi/) abin karɓa ne ga yawancin matakai; ana tsammanin [binciken amfanin kuɗi](../binciken-amfanin-kuɗi/) a mafi girman haɗari. ESF yana ayyana **kuɗin shaida na shiga kasuwa** — tsara masa kasafi kamar kowane kuɗin gini.

## Lissafin

Babu dabaru — tebur ne na shawara. Lissafin aiki na kasuwanci ne:

```
Evidence investment required = f(tier)
  Tier A: documentation + assurance ≈ £10k–50k
  Tier B: observational/comparative user-benefit study ≈ £50k–250k
  Tier C: RCT-grade comparative study + economic model ≈ £250k–£2M+

Position your product's claims deliberately: claiming "supports clinical
decisions" instead of "informs patients" moves you a tier and can 10× the bill.
```

## Misali da aka lissafa

Mai kera manhajar tunatar da magani yana tunanin ƙara fasalin shawarar daidaita adadin magani.

- A matsayin manhajar tunatarwa: **Tier B** — binciken ƙungiya da ke nuna ingantuwar bin ƙa'ida ya isa.
- Tare da shawarwarin adadi: **Tier C** — shaidar tasirin kwatance (mai yiwuwa RCT a kan kulawar yau da kullum) tare da binciken tattalin arziki.

Idan RCT zai kai £600k kuma ƙarin kuɗin shiga na fasalin adadi £200k/shekara ne, fasalin dole ya riƙe daraja na shekaru 3+ kafin kuɗin shaida ya daidaita — shawarar samfur da take da kamanni daban gaba ɗaya da zarar an yi wa matakin ESF farashi. Ƙungiyoyi da yawa suna fitar da samfurin Tier B su jinkirta da'awar Tier C har sai an sami kuɗi.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

ESF shi ne tsarin shugabanci mafi sauƙin canjawa a wannan ma'ajiya: **buƙatun shaida masu matakan haɗari don karɓar kayan aiki**. Sigar cikin gida: na'urar tsara lamba tana buƙatar nuni (Tier A); kayan aikin ƙwazo da ke da'awar ceton sa'o'i yana buƙatar gwaji da aka auna (Tier B); ƙofar AI da ke toshe turawa ta atomatik ko rubuta lambar asibiti ta atomatik tana buƙatar shaida irin ta gwajin sarrafawa kafin yaɗawa a ƙungiya gaba ɗaya (Tier C). Shaida mai daidaito tana hana hanyoyin gazawa biyu — ɗaiɗaita ƙananan kayan aiki da ƙa'idodi, da fitar da masu muhimmanci bisa jin daɗi kawai. Duba kuma [hanyar gaggawa ta DiGA](../hanyar-gaggawa-ta-diga-ta-jamus/) don kari na "karɓa na wucin gadi da wa'adin shaida".

## Tarkuna

- **Rarraba matakin kuskure bisa fata** — masu ƙa'ida da masu saye suna rarrabawa bisa abin da samfurin *yake yi*, ba abin da tallace-tallace ke cewa ba.
- **Shaida da aka gina bayan samfur**: dasa RCT a kan samfurin da aka riga aka fitar ba tare da kayan aunawa ko daidaiton rashin tabbas ba yana da jinkiri kuma sau da yawa ba zai yiwu ba.
- **Cika ESF a manta da sauran**: ESF yana tare da DTAC (amincin asibiti, kariyar bayanai, haɗin kai) kuma, ga AI, izinin ƙa'ida — duba [tantance AI ta hukumomin ƙa'ida](../tantance-ai-ta-hukumomin-ƙa-ida/).
