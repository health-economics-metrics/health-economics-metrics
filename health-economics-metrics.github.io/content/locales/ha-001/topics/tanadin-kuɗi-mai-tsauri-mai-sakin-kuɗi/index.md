# Tanadin Kuɗi Mai Tsauri Mai Sakin Kuɗi (Kare Gibi)

Tanadin kuɗi mai tsauri mai sakin kuɗi layukan abubuwa ne da asibiti zai iya **share su daga kasafin kuɗin wata mai zuwa** saboda manhajarka. A idon ma'aikacin lissafin kuɗi mai tsauri — da asusun asibiti da ke da gibi — wannan ita ce kawai nau'in fa'ida da ke ƙidayuwa gaba ɗaya.

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

Yawancin asusun NHS suna aiki a ƙarƙashin tsare-tsaren murmurewa daga gibi tare da bincike mai tsanani na kowane layin kashe kuɗi. A wannan yanayi, fa'idodin ƙarfi da ingantawar inganci — komai gaskiyarsu — ba sa rufe gibin; kuɗi ne kaɗai. Samfurin manhaja da zai iya tabbatar da cewa yana share layukan kasafin kuɗi *yana ba da kuɗin kansa ta mahangar CFO*, wanda ke canza sayen: tattaunawar ta daina zama "za mu iya biyan wannan?" ta zama "za mu iya biyan rashin yin sa?". Wannan daftarin shi ne ƙarshen mai kaifi mai fuskantar gibi na [tanadi mai sakin kuɗi da wanda ba ya sakin kuɗi](../tanadi-mai-sakin-kuɗi-da-wanda-ba-ya-sakin-kuɗi/).

## Lissafin

Mafi amintaccen manufar kuɗi mai tsauri ta NHS ita ce **ma'aikatan wucin gadi masu ƙimar fiye**. Asusun asibiti suna rufe giɓi da ma'aikatan "Bank" na ciki (ana biyan su kusan ƙimar yau da kullum) da ma'aikatan "Agency" na waje (sau da yawa 2–3× ƙimar Agenda for Change, da iyaka amma ana karya shi akai-akai ga ayyukan da ba su da yawa).

```
Hard saving = premium shifts avoided × (premium rate − substantive rate)
            + overtime hours avoided × overtime premium
            + external contracts cancelled × contract value

Mechanism requirement: name the specific budget line and the manager who
will confirm its reduction. If no one can point to the line, it isn't hard cash.
```

## Misali da aka lissafa

Ma'aikaciyar jinya ta Band 6 tana rasa ~sa'a 1/aiki zuwa nauyin gudanarwa; rubuta bayanai akai-akai yana zubewa bayan ƙarshen aiki zuwa ƙarin lokaci, kuma ɗakuna suna yin rajistar ƙarin kulawa ta Bank don cim ma rubuta bayanai.

Manhaja tana mayar da wannan sa'a zuwa aikin da aka tsara a tsakanin ma'aikatan jinya 300:

```
Overtime avoided:   300 nurses × 2.5 paid overtime hrs/week × £8 premium × 46 wks
                    ≈ £276,000/year
Bank/agency shifts: 15 catch-up shifts/week × £180 premium × 52
                    ≈ £140,400/year
Hard cash total     ≈ £416,000/year against a licence cost of ~£150,000
```

Kowane fam za a iya dubawa a kan tsarin e-rostering da albashi — daidai yadda ya kamata a tabbatar da fa'idar, kowane wata, ta hanyar [gane fa'idodi](../gane-fa-idodi/). (Samfuran ma'aikata da aka wallafa na NHS sun yi da'awar rabo har zuwa £11+ da aka ceta ga kowane £1 da aka kashe a wannan hanya; a ɗauki duk irin wannan rabo a matsayin hasashe ga bayanan jadawalin asusunka, ba gaskiya mai ɗaukuwa ba.)

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Takwarorin injiniya na ƙarin kuɗin hukuma su ne sayayyar damuwa ta ƙungiyar kanta: ƙimar ranar ɗan kwangila da ke rufe giɓin isarwa, ƙarin lokacin da lamari ya jawo, kwangilolin tallafi na gaggawa, da firgicin farashin spot na gajimare. Manhajar ƙwazo da ke da'awar kuɗi mai tsauri ya kamata ta kai wa waɗannan layuka da horo iri ɗaya — a ambaci layin kasafin kuɗi, mai mallaka, da watan da yake ƙanƙancewa. Duk sauran abin da take isarwa ƙarfi ne ([ƙarfin samar da daraja](../ƙarfin-samar-da-daraja/)) ko inganci: na gaske, masu daraja, kuma daban.

## Tarkuna

- **Kiran ƙarfi "tanadi"** — mai kashe sahihanci nan take tare da kuɗi; duba rarrabuwa a [tanadi mai sakin kuɗi da wanda ba ya sakin kuɗi](../tanadi-mai-sakin-kuɗi-da-wanda-ba-ya-sakin-kuɗi/).
- **Rabon samfurin mai sayarwa a matsayin gaskiyar gida** (matsalar £11:£1) — a sake gina samfurin a kan bayanan jadawalin asusun.
- **Rikicewar sau ɗaya da maimaituwa**: kwangilar da aka soke tana adana darajarta sau ɗaya a shekara, ba sau ɗaya ba; aikin da aka share yana adana albashi ne kawai muddin yana cikin sharewa.
