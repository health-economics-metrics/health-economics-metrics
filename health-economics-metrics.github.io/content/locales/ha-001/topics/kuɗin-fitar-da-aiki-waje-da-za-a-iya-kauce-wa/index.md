# Kuɗin Fitar da Aiki Waje da Za a Iya Kauce Wa

Idan asusun asibiti ba zai iya cimma maƙasudai da ƙarfin cikinsa ba, yana sayen ƙarfi a farashi mai tsada: ƙarin lokacin aiki na ƙarshen mako ga ma'aikatansa, ko fitar da tiyata zuwa masu bayarwa masu zaman kansu. Darajar tattalin arziki ta manhajar da ke 'yantar da ƙarfi ta ƙunshi **kuɗin da za a iya kauce wa na wannan aiki mai tsada**.

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

A ƙarƙashin matsin murmurewa na tiyatar zaɓi, asusun asibiti akai-akai suna biyan farashin kasuwa na masu zaman kansu (sau da yawa 1.2–1.5× farashin tsarin NHS) ko ƙimar waiting-list-initiative mai tsada ga likitocinsu don jerin aikin ƙarshen mako. Ba kamar da'awar ƙarfi ta al'ada ba, kaucewa fitar da aiki waje yana **sakin kuɗi**: da gaske ba a rubuta kuɗin da za a aika wa mai bayarwa mai zaman kansa ba. Wannan ya sa ta zama ɗaya daga cikin mafi ƙarfin layukan fa'ida ga manhajar da ke ƙara fitarwar cikin gida — kuma ɗaya daga cikin mafi sauƙin tabbatarwa, domin kuɗin fitar da aiki waje layin kasafin kuɗi ne da ake gani tuni.

## Lissafin

```
Avoidable outsourcing cost = activity moved in-house × (outsourced unit price
                             − internal marginal cost per case)

Internal marginal cost: consumables + variable staffing for the extra activity
— the fixed estate is already paid for (see marginal-vs-average-cost.md).
```

Da'awar tana buƙatar ƙarfin cikin da aka 'yantar ya shanye aikin da gaske: zaman ɗakin tiyata, gadaje, da ma'aikata duk dole su kasance (ƙuntatawa mai ɗaure tana mulki — ka'idar ƙuntatawa kuma).

## Misali da aka lissafa

Wani asusun asibiti yana fitar da tiyatar ido ta cataract 800 a shekara a £900 kowacce: £720,000/shekara na kuɗin waje, da farashin tsarin ~£750.

Manhajar tsara jadawalin ɗakin tiyata (inganta jeri, cike giɓi daga sokewa, bin lokacin canjin) tana ƙara amfani da ɗakin tiyata na ciki isasshe don dawo da tiyata 500:

```
Internal marginal cost per case ≈ £350 (consumables + sessional staffing)
Saving = 500 × (900 − 350) = £275,000/year — cash-releasing
Remaining outsourcing: 300 × £900 = £270,000 (was £720,000)
```

Kuɗin manhaja £90,000/shekara → net ≈ **+£185,000/shekara na kuɗin da za a iya ajiyewa a banki**, ban da fa'idodin inganci na ciki da horo na ajiye aikin a gida.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Makamancin kai tsaye shi ne **ƙarin kuɗin ɗan kwangila da mashawarta**: lokacin da ƙarfin injiniya na ciki ba zai iya cika alƙawura ba, ƙungiyoyi suna sayen ƙarfin waje a 1.5–3× na ƙimar ciki da aka ɗora. Jarin dandamali da ƙwazo da ke ɗaga fitarwar ciki ya kamata ya yi da'awar kuɗin ɗan kwangila da aka kauce wa daidai kamar yadda aka bayyana — ƙimar rana ta waje ragewa kuɗin ƙari na ciki, ninka aikin da aka dawo da shi — domin yana ɗaya daga cikin ƙalilan layukan sakin kuɗi na gaske a shari'ar kasuwanci ta ƙwazon mai haɓakawa. Gargaɗin yana nan: ƙarfin cikin dole ya wanzu da gaske kuma a tsara shi zuwa aikin da aka dawo da shi, in ba haka ba da'awar almara ce.

## Tarkuna

- **Da'awar dawo da aiki ba tare da cikakken sarkar ƙarfi ba** — likitocin tiyata da aka 'yantar amma babu zaman ɗakin tiyata (ko injiniyoyi da aka 'yantar amma babu isasshen lokacin gudanar da samfuri) ba ya dawo da komai.
- **Kwatanta farashin fitarwa waje da matsakaicin kuɗin ciki** maimakon kuɗin ƙari — yana rage tanadi, abin mamaki; kuɗaɗen tsayayyu suna nan ko ta yaya.
- **Rashin daidaiton inganci/rikitarwa**: lamuran da ake fitarwa waje yawanci masu sauƙi ne; dawo da su yana canza haɗin lamuran ciki da kuɗin naúra.
