# Farashin Damar da Aka Bari

Farashin damar da aka bari shi ne darajar mafi kyawun zaɓin da kake rasawa idan ka ɗauki wani albarka. A tsarin lafiya mai kasafin kuɗi tsayayye, kashe fam miliyan £1 a kan abu ɗaya na nufin fam miliyan £1 na lafiyar da *ba* a samar ba a wani wuri dabam.

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

Farashin damar da aka bari shi ne manufa mafi zurfi a tattalin arzikin lafiya, kuma wadda injiniyoyin manhaja ke tsallakewa fiye da kowacce. Kasafin kuɗin lafiya tsayayye ne a kowace shekara, don haka ba a taɓa ba da kuɗin sabuwar fasaha daga "ƙarin" kuɗi ba — tana maye gurbin wani abu ne. Tambayar da mai biya yake yi a zahiri ba ita ce "ko wannan yana da kyau?" ba, sai "ko wannan ya fi abin da kuɗin nan ɗaya yake saya a yanzu?"

Wannan ne ya sa iyakokin tasirin kuɗi suke wanzuwa: iyakar ƙiyasi ne na lafiyar da kuɗi ke saya a gefen tsarin na yanzu. Duba [iyakokin shirin biya](../iyakokin-shirin-biya/).

## Lissafin

Babu dabara ɗaya; farashin damar da aka bari horo ne na kwatantawa:

```
Opportunity cost of choosing A = value of best forgone alternative B
Net gain from A = value(A) − value(B)
```

Ma'aunin shaida: Claxton da sauransu (2015) sun ƙiyasta cewa NHS tana samar da QALY ɗaya a kan kusan **£13,000** a gefen tsarin. Don haka £13,000 da aka kashe a kan fasahar da ke samar da ƙasa da QALY ɗaya na sa ƙasa ta zama *ƙasa* lafiya, ko da fasahar "tana aiki."

## Misali da aka lissafa

Kasafin kuɗin sauyi na wani asusun NHS zai iya biyan ɗaya ne kawai daga cikin waɗannan:

- **Zaɓi A**: manhajar tsara jadawalin aiki ta e-rostering — tana adana £400,000/shekara a kuɗin ma'aikatan wucin gadi na hukumomi.
- **Zaɓi B**: manhajar haɗa sallamar marasa lafiya — tana adana kwanakin gado 2,000/shekara. A kan kuɗin ƙari na kusan £150 ga kowace ranar gado da aka 'yantar da gaske, wannan ya kai £300,000/shekara, tare da magani da wuri ga marasa lafiya da ke jira.

Ba da kuɗi ga A na nufin barin B. Farashin damar da aka bari na A shi ne £300,000 na B tare da fa'idar marasa lafiya; hujjar *net* ta A ita ce bambanci kawai, ba £400,000 da aka sanar ba. Duk wata shari'ar kasuwanci da ta kwatanta shawara da "kada a yi komai" maimakon mafi kyawun madadin tana ƙara darajarta fiye da gaskiya.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Ƙarfin injiniya ma kasafin kuɗi ne tsayayye — wuraren taswirar hanya, ba fam ba. Ƙungiyar dandamali da ke ba da kuɗi ga kayan aiki A da ke adana sa'o'in injiniya a £500/sa'a alhali kayan aiki B yana isar da hakan a £200/sa'a tana lalata ƙarfi, daidai kamar yadda tsarin lafiya da ke biyan maganin £40,000/QALY yake maye gurbin kulawar £13,000/QALY. Wannan horo yana wucewa kai tsaye:

- Kullum ka ambaci abin kwatantawa ("idan aka kwatanta da me?").
- Ka ƙimanta lokacin injiniya da abin da zai samar in ba haka ba, ba da albashi kawai ba.
- Ka ɗauki "har yanzu muna da kasafin kuɗi" a matsayin farkon bincike, ba ƙarshensa ba.

## Tarkuna

- **Kwatantawa da komai.** Abin kwatantawa daidai shi ne amfani na gaba mafi kyau na kuɗin, wanda da wuya ya kasance "kada a yi komai."
- **Zaton lokacin da aka ceta ba shi da farashin damar da aka bari.** Lokacin da aka ceta yana da daraja ne kawai idan aka mayar da shi zuwa wani abu mai daraja — duba [tanadi mai sakin kuɗi da wanda ba ya sakin kuɗi](../tanadi-mai-sakin-kuɗi-da-wanda-ba-ya-sakin-kuɗi/).
- **Watsi da maye gurbi.** "Kasafin kuɗi zai faɗaɗa ya isa" kusan ba gaskiya ba ne a hidimar lafiya ta ƙasa cikin shekara.
- **Rashin lura da wace hanya ke ƙimanta albarkatun da aka maye gurbinsu.** Musamman ga asarar aiki — daga rashin lafiya, nakasa, ko ma'aikacin da ke tafiya — duba [tsarin jarin ɗan adam da hanyar kuɗin gogayya](../tsarin-jarin-ɗan-adam-da-hanyar-kuɗin-gogayya/), sigar wannan manufa ta musamman ga kuɗin aiki.
