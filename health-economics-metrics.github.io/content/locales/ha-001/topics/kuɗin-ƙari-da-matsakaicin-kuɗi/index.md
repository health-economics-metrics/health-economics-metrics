# Kuɗin Ƙari da Matsakaicin Kuɗi

Matsakaicin kuɗi shi ne jimlar kuɗi da aka raba da naúrorin da aka samar. Kuɗin ƙari shi ne kuɗin samar da naúra *ɗaya ƙari*. Ya kamata a yanke shawara bisa kuɗin ƙari — amma kuɗin naúra da aka wallafa kusan koyaushe matsakaici ne.

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

Kuskure mafi yawa a shari'o'in kasuwanci na lafiyar dijital shi ne ƙimanta albarkatun da aka ceta a **matsakaicin** kuɗinsa alhali ainihin tanadi shi ne kuɗin **ƙari**. Ranar gadon asibiti tana da matsakaicin kuɗi (da aka sha gaba ɗaya) na £400+, amma 'yantar da ranar gado ɗaya ba ya ceton £400 — ginin, dumama, da yawancin kuɗin ma'aikata suna ci gaba. Kuɗin da aka saki a zahiri na iya zama £50–£150 sai dai idan an 'yantar da isassun gadaje don rufe ɗaki.

## Lissafin

```
Average cost:  AC = TC / Q
Marginal cost: MC = dTC/dQ   (cost of one more/one fewer unit)

TC = total cost, Q = quantity
```

Kuɗaɗen tsayayyu suna sa MC < AC ga rage ƙarfi, kuma MC na iya kusantar sifili lokacin da akwai ƙarfin da ya wuce. Da'awar tanadi ya kamata ta yi amfani da:

```
True saving = ΔQ × MC          (small changes)
True saving = step change in TC (large changes that cross a capacity threshold, e.g. closing a ward)
```

## Misali da aka lissafa

Manhajarka tana rage matsakaicin tsawon zama, tana 'yantar da kwanakin gado 1,000/shekara a asusun asibiti.

- **Da'awa maras zurfi**: 1,000 × £400 matsakaicin kuɗi = **£400,000 da aka ceta**. Ba daidai ba.
- **Da'awar ƙari**: kuɗin canji ga kowace ranar gado (abinci, wanki, kayan amfani, wasu sassaucin jinya) ≈ £120. Tanadi = 1,000 × £120 = **£120,000**, *tare da* darajar ƙarfin da aka 'yantar idan aka sake cika gadaje da marasa lafiya na zaɓi masu jira (kuɗin shiga a ƙarƙashin biyan bisa aiki, ko rage jerin jira).
- **Da'awar canjin mataki**: idan asusun ya 'yantar da kwanakin gado 7,300/shekara (ɗaki mai gadaje 20) zai iya rufe ɗakin a zahiri: ma'aikata + gudanarwa ≈ £1.5 miliyan/shekara na kuɗi na gaske. Yanzu lissafin matsakaicin kuɗi ya fi kusa da gaskiya.

Shiga tsakani ɗaya, lambobi uku da za a iya kare, bisa ko canjin ya haye matakin ƙarfi.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Tattalin arzikin gajimare yankin kuɗin ƙari ne na asali:

- Kuɗin ƙari na gudanar da CI ɗaya ƙari a kan ƙarfin da aka riga aka ajiye ≈ £0, alhali matsakaicin kuɗi ga kowane gudanarwa (jimlar kashe kuɗin dandamali ÷ gudanarwa) na iya zama fam. Tsarin cajin da ke lissafin matsakaicin kuɗi yana tura ƙungiyoyi su yi amfani da ƙasa da ƙarfin tarayya da ke kyauta a ƙari.
- Akasin haka, "mun ceci 30% na lissafi" yana sakin kuɗi ne kawai idan an dakatar da misalai ko an rage ajiyar wuri a zahiri — sigar manhaja ta tarkon ranar gado. Duba [tanadi mai sakin kuɗi da wanda ba ya sakin kuɗi](../tanadi-mai-sakin-kuɗi-da-wanda-ba-ya-sakin-kuɗi/).

## Tarkuna

- **Ƙimanta ƙarfi a matsakaicin kuɗi** a gabatar da shi a matsayin kuɗi (na gargajiya).
- **Zaton kuɗin ƙari tsayayye ne.** Yana hawa a iyakokin ƙarfi (rufe ɗakuna, matakan lasisi, alƙawuran misalai da aka ajiye).
- **Amfani da kuɗin ƙari ga shawarwarin faɗaɗa amma matsakaici ga raguwa** a shari'a ɗaya — a zaɓa bisa ainihin shawarar.
