# Adadin Rashin Halartar Alƙawari (DNA)

Adadin DNA shi ne kaso na alƙawuran da aka yi rajista inda majiyyaci bai halarta ba kuma bai soke ba. An biya likita, ɗaki, da lokacin; babu abin da ke faruwa. Shi ne mafi tsantsar ma'aunin ɓarna a kula da lafiya — kuma ɗaya daga cikin waɗanda manhaja ta fi iya gyarawa.

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

Alkaluman NHS England (2019): alƙawuran likitan gida da aka rasa sun wuce miliyan 15/shekara a ~£30 kowanne — sama da **£216M/shekara** — kuma DNA na asibiti na waje yana kusan miliyan 8/shekara (~6.4% na alƙawura) a matsakaicin ~**£160** ga kowane lokacin da aka rasa. Domin kuɗin ƙarin tunatarwa kobo ne kuma darajar da aka dawo da ita cikakken lokacin asibiti ne da ma'aikata ke ciki, rage DNA yana da wasu mafi kyawun lissafin ROI a lafiyar dijital, wanda ya sa tunatarwar SMS, sauƙin sake yin alƙawari, da yin rajista fiye da kima na hasashe suka kasance cikin nasarorin lafiyar dijital na farko da aka tabbatar.

## Lissafin

```
DNA rate = DNAs / booked appointments × 100

Value of reduction = appointments × ΔDNA rate × value per recovered slot

value per recovered slot: the slot is refilled (activity value / waiting-list
reduction) or it is not (staff time partially reusable) — mechanism matters,
as with bed-days-saved.md.
```

## Misali da aka lissafa

Sashen marasa lafiya na waje: alƙawura 200,000/shekara, adadin DNA 8%. Hidimar tunatarwa-da-sake-alƙawari (SMS tare da sake yin alƙawari ta taɓawa ɗaya, bayanin sufuri, tsare-tsare masu samuwa) tana rage DNA zuwa 5.5%.

```
Recovered slots = 200,000 × 0.025 = 5,000/year
Refilled from the waiting list at ~£160 average outpatient value:
  5,000 × £160 = £800,000/year of recovered activity
Service cost: 200,000 × £0.40 = £80,000/year

Return ≈ 10:1, plus 5,000 waiting-list patients seen sooner
(see waiting-list-impact.md and referral-to-treatment.md).
```

Girman tasirin (maki 2.5) na zahiri ne: gwajin tunatarwa akai-akai suna nuna rage DNA na dangi na 25–40%.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

- **Wannan matsala ce ta tsarin tsara jadawali**: tunatarwa, sake yin alƙawari da kai, cike jerin jira ta atomatik daga sokewa, da samfuran hasashen rashin halarta da ke tuƙa yin rajista biyu da aka nufa. Kowanne injiniyancin manhaja ne na yau da kullum mai shari'ar tattalin arziki mai tsabta ƙwarai.
- **Takwaran injiniya**: rashin halartar ƙarfin da aka ajiye — wuraren CI da aka yi rajista amma ba a amfani da su ba, ƙarfin gajimare da aka ajiye, ɗakunan taro, kwamitocin tambayoyi. Tattalin arziki yana wucewa: tunatarwa mai arha ta atomatik (ko sakin ajiyar da ba a amfani da ita ta atomatik) tana dawo da ƙarfin da aka ɗaure mai tsada.
- **Samfoti na ɗabi'ar hasashe**: samfuran hasashen rashin halarta da aka horar a kan bayanan halarta suna ɗauke da talauci da shingen samun dama; amfani da su don *rage fifiko* ga waɗanda ake tsammanin ba za su halarta ba yana ƙara rashin adalci, amfani da su don *tallafa* halartar (taimakon sufuri, madadin waya) yana rage shi. Duba [isa da adalci](../isa-da-adalci/).

## Tarkuna

- **Ƙidaya wanda aka soke kuma aka sake yin alƙawari a matsayin darajar da aka dawo da ita sau biyu.**
- **Ƙimanta lokutan da aka dawo da su waɗanda ba a sake cika su ba** — lokacin da babu kowa wanda aka aika masa da tunatarwa har yanzu babu kowa.
- **Bin DNA zuwa sifili**: maki na ƙarshe na DNA marasa lafiya ne masu fuskantar shingaye na gaske; hanyoyin hukunci (sallama bayan DNA N) suna rage ma'aunin ta barin marasa lafiya.
