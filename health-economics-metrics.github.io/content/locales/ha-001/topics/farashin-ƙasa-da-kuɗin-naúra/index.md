# Farashin Ƙasa da Kuɗin Naúra

NHS tana biyan masu bayarwa don aiki a ƙarƙashin jerin farashin ƙasa mai tushen ƙa'idodi — a tarihi National Tariff / Payment by Results, wanda **NHS Payment Scheme (NHSPS)** ya maye gurbinsa a 1 Afrilu 2023. A bayan farashin akwai kayan aikin ƙididdige kuɗin naúra na ƙasa: **National Cost Collection (NCC)** da littafin **PSSRU Unit Costs of Health and Social Care**.

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

Waɗannan su ne masu rabawa na kowace amintacciyar shari'ar kasuwanci ta NHS. Idan da'awa ta ce "ziyarar asibiti ta waje tana da daraja £160" ko "sa'ar ma'aikaciyar jinya ta Band 6 tana kashe £31", waɗannan lambobi suna zuwa daga wannan kayan aiki — kuma amfani da alkaluman hukuma maimakon na ƙirƙira shi ne ke sa kimantawa masu zaman kansu su kwatantu kuma ƙungiyoyin kuɗi su ba da haɗin kai. Ga mai sayarwa, farashin kuma yana ayyana ɓangaren *kuɗin shiga*: aikin da manhajarka ke ba da dama (ƙarin asibitoci, gadaje da aka cike) ana ƙimanta shi a farashin tsari.

## Lissafin

```
Tariff price per unit of activity (HRG-coded spell, outpatient attendance)
  = national average unit cost (from NCC) × Market Forces Factor (local adjustment)
  under NHSPS: blended fixed + variable ("aligned payment and incentive") elements

NCC unit cost = trust-reported total cost of an activity type / activity volume
                (built on Patient-Level Information and Costing Systems, PLICS)

PSSRU compendium: ~80 standard unit costs (GP consultation, nurse-hour by band,
ED attendance…) — the default source in UK economic evaluations.
```

## Misali da aka lissafa

Manhajarka tana 'yantar da sa'a 1/rana ta lokacin ma'aikaciyar jinya ta Band 6 a shekarar aiki ta kwana 250:

```
PSSRU-based Band 6 cost incl. overheads ≈ £31/hour (check current edition)
Capacity value = 250 × £31 = £7,750/nurse/year (non-cash-releasing)
```

A madadin haka ma'aikaciyar jinyar tana gudanar da ƙarin alƙawuran bi-da-bi na asibiti na waje 2/rana a ~£160 na darajar tsari: 500 × £160 = **£80,000/shekara na aikin da aka ba da kuɗi** — bambanci na ninki goma a darajar da aka yi da'awa bisa sake turawa, duka daga kuɗin naúra na hukuma. Duk da'awar biyun ana iya dubawa domin an wallafa masu rabawa; wannan shi ne ma'anar gaba ɗaya.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Wannan tsarin **littafin farashin ciki** ne. Tattalin arzikin lafiya na Birtaniya yana aiki saboda kowace kimantawa tana amfani da kuɗin naúra da aka wallafa iri ɗaya; ƙungiyoyin injiniya galibi ba su da wannan, don haka kowace shari'ar kasuwanci tana ƙirƙirar kuɗinta na sa'ar injiniya, lamari, aikawa. Ƙungiyar dandamali za ta iya wallafa irin wannan littafi daidai — kuɗin da aka ɗora ga kowace sa'ar injiniya bisa mataki, ga kowane lamari bisa tsanani, ga kowane minti na gini — kuma ta buƙaci amfani da shi a duk shawarwari. Tsarin cajin ciki/nuni yana maimaita sanannun gazawar farashin ma: farashin matsakaicin kuɗi yana tura wasan adadi, biyan tsayayye yana tura ƙarancin tanadi. Juyin NHSPS daga biyan aiki kawai zuwa gauraye tsayayye+mai canji shekaru ashirin ne na darussan ƙirar ƙarfafawa don farashin dandamali na ciki.

## Tarkuna

- **Alkaluma da suka tsufa**: farashin NCC, PSSRU, da NHSPS suna sabuntuwa kowace shekara — a yi wa kowace lamba kwanan wata.
- **Farashin tsari ≠ kuɗi**: farashi matsakaicin ƙasa ne tare da gyare-gyare; kuɗin ƙarinka na gida ya bambanta (duba [kuɗin ƙari da matsakaicin kuɗi](../kuɗin-ƙari-da-matsakaicin-kuɗi/)).
- **Ƙimanta ƙarfi a farashin tsari ba tare da hanya** ta isar da da samun biyan ƙarin aikin ba.
