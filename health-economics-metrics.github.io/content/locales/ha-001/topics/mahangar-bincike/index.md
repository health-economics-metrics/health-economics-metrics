# Mahangar Bincike

Mahanga tana ƙayyade kuɗin *wane* da fa'idar *wane* ke ƙidayuwa a binciken tattalin arziki: na mai biya, na mai bayarwa, ko na al'umma gabaɗaya. Shiga tsakani ɗaya na iya zama mai ban sha'awa daga mahanga ɗaya kuma mara kyau daga wata.

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

Kowane kimantawar tattalin arziki dole ya bayyana mahangarsa tun farko, domin mahangar tana ƙayyade waɗanne layukan abu ke wanzuwa:

- **Mahangar mai biya** (misali, mai ba da umarni na NHS, mai inshora): kuɗin da mai biya ke mayarwa kawai.
- **Mahangar mai bayarwa** (misali, asusun asibiti): kuɗin isarwa na ciki, ma'aikata, gine-gine.
- **Mahangar al'umma**: komai — har da lokacin majiyyaci, tafiya, kulawa ta iyali ta ba bisa tsari ba, da asarar aiki ga masu ɗaukar aiki.

Ƙa'idar tunani ta NICE tana amfani da mahangar **NHS da Personal Social Services (PSS)** ga kuɗi. Kwamitin Biyu na Amurka kan Tasirin Kuɗi yana ba da shawarar bayar da rahoton binciken ɓangaren kula da lafiya da na al'umma duka tare da "jerin tasiri" da ke lissafa abin da aka haɗa.

## Lissafin

Babu dabara — ka'idar iyakance da ake amfani da ita kafin kowane lissafi:

```
Included cost/benefit categories = f(perspective)
```

Duba mai amfani: a gina teburin jerin tasiri mai jere ɗaya ga kowane kuɗi/fa'ida da shafi ɗaya ga kowace mahanga, a yi alama a ƙwayoyin da ke ƙidayuwa.

## Misali da aka lissafa

Manhajar duba alamu tana karkatar da ziyarar likitan gida 10,000 a shekara zuwa kula da kai.

- **Mai biya (NHS)**: yana adana 10,000 × £42 na kowace shawarar likita = **£420,000/shekara** — mai kyau ƙwarai.
- **Mai bayarwa (asibitin likitan gida)**: idan ana biyan asibitoci ta kaso-ga-kai, kuɗin shigarsu bai canza ba amma aikinsu ya ragu — mai kyau kaɗan.
- **Al'umma**: a ƙara tafiya da lokacin jiran marasa lafiya da aka ceta, a ce 10,000 × sa'o'i 2 × £15/sa'a = £300,000 na darajar lokaci; amma a cire cutarwa idan 2% aka tabbatar musu da ƙarya kuma suka dawo daga baya, sun fi rashin lafiya, a 200 × £3,000 = £600,000 na ƙarin magani. Net na al'umma: 420,000 + 300,000 − 600,000 = **£120,000/shekara** — mai kyau, amma zaton aminci ya mamaye shi.

Manhaja ɗaya, amsoshi uku dabam-dabam. Bayyana mahangar ne ke sa lambobin su zama masu kwatantuwa da gaskiya.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

ROI na kayan aiki da dandamali yana da mahanga ma:

- **Kasafin ƙungiya ("mai biya")**: shin kuɗin lasisi ya shiga cibiyar kuɗina?
- **Ƙungiyar dandamali ("mai bayarwa")**: jimillar kuɗi har da haɗawa, tallafi, da kulawa.
- **Kamfani ("al'umma")**: a haɗa tasirin abokin ciniki, tasirin tsaro na waje, da lokacin kowace ƙungiya da abin ya shafa.

Kayan CI da yake arha ga ƙungiyar da ta saya amma ke tura aikin ƙaura zuwa wasu ƙungiyoyi 40 shi ne sigar manhaja ta tura kuɗi — wanda ake gani daga faɗin mahanga kawai. A bayyana mahangar a kowace shari'ar kasuwanci; masu dubawa ba za su iya ƙalubalantar zaton da ba su gani ba.

## Tarkuna

- **Sauyin mahanga a ɓoye**: ƙidaya fa'idodin al'umma amma kuɗin mai biya kawai yana sa komai ya zama mai tasirin kuɗi.
- **Ƙidaya sau biyu** lokacin da aka haɗa mahanga (misali, ƙidaya alƙawarin likitan gida da aka ceta a matsayin tanadin mai biya da tanadin lokacin majiyyaci alhali lambar mai biya ta riga ta haɗa lokacin ma'aikata).
- **Watsi da tura kuɗi**: "tanadi" da kawai ke tura kuɗi zuwa marasa lafiya, masu kulawa, ko wani sashe.
