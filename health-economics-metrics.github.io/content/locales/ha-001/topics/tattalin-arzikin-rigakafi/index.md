# Tattalin Arzikin Rigakafi

Tattalin arzikin shiga tsakani kafin cuta ta faru ko ta ci gaba. Babban bincike abin mamaki ne: **yawancin rigakafi ba ya ceton kuɗi** — yana sayen lafiya a farashi mai kyau. Binciken NEJM mai tarihi na Cohen, Neumann da Weinstein ya gano ƙasa da 20% na matakan rigakafi ne ke ceton kuɗi net; sauran masu tasirin kuɗi ne a mafi kyau.

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

"Rigakafi yana ceton kuɗi" ita ce ƙarya da aka fi maimaitawa a manufofin lafiya, kuma shari'o'in kasuwanci da aka gina a kanta masana tattalin arzikin lafiya na rushe su. Tsarin gaskiya: rigakafi yana kashe kuɗi yanzu (bincika dukan al'umma, magance abubuwan haɗari a mutanen da ba za su taɓa rashin lafiya ba) kuma yana dawo da lafiya daga baya — yawanci a *kyakkyawan* kuɗi ga kowane QALY, wani lokaci a tanadi, wani lokaci a farashi mai muni. Sanin wane tsari kake ciki shi ne binciken. Bambancin yana da muhimmanci a kasuwanci: samfurin rigakafi da aka sayar a matsayin "yana ceton NHS kuɗi" yana gayyatar bincike da zai faɗi; idan aka sayar a matsayin "yana sayen QALY a £4,000" zai iya yin nasara a kan gaskiya ɗaya. Duba [shiga tsakani da wuri](../shiga-tsakani-da-wuri/) don sigar cikin hanya ɗaya. Kafin ƙididdige kuɗin shirin rigakafi, [kaso mai alaƙa da jama'a](../kaso-mai-alaƙa-da-jama-a/) yana amsa tambayar girma tukuna — nawa na nauyin cutar da ake nufi abin haɗarin da shirin ke magancewa zai iya kawarwa.

## Lissafin

```
Net cost of prevention (per person) =
    intervention cost × everyone treated
  − downstream costs avoided × the few who would have progressed
  (both discounted — the avoided costs are years away; see
   discounting-and-time-preference.md)

Cost-saving requires: intervention cost < P(progression) × avoided cost × discount factor
Cost-effective requires only: net cost / QALYs gained < threshold
```

Rashin fahimtar rigakafi: kuɗin shiga tsakani yana ninkuwa a dukan al'umma; fa'idodi suna zuwa kawai ga kaɗan na yanayin da ba a yi ba.

## Misali da aka lissafa

Manhajar sarrafa hawan jini da aka bayar ga manya 100,000 masu haɗari, £25/mutum/shekara. A cikin shekaru 10 tana hana bugun jini 400 (kowanne yana kashe £45,000 da aka rage daraja, kuma QALY 3 da aka rasa).

```
Cost:    100,000 × £25 × 10 yrs (discounted ≈ ×8.3) ≈ £20.8M
Offsets: 400 × £45,000 = £18.0M
Net cost ≈ £2.8M — NOT cost-saving

QALYs gained = 400 × 3 = 1,200
Cost per QALY = 2.8M / 1,200 ≈ £2,300/QALY — outstandingly cost-effective
```

Shirin ɗaya, gaskiya biyu: yana rasa £2.8M a kuɗi kuma yana sayen lafiya a kashi ɗaya cikin goma na iyakar NICE. Ba da kuɗi saboda lamba ta biyu; kada ka taɓa alƙawarin ta farko.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Ingancin da aka matsa gaba tattalin arzikin rigakafi ne, tare da gargaɗi. Bita, gwaje-gwaje, da bincike a tsaye suna sanya kuɗi a kan *kowane* canji don kama matsaloli a kaɗan da za su ci gaba zuwa abubuwan da suka faru a samarwa. Lanƙwasar kuɗin lahani (10–100× ta mataki) tana taka rawar kuɗin bugun jini — kuma ƙarshen gaskiya yana kama da na lafiya: matsa-gaba yawanci yana da *tasirin kuɗi*, ba lallai *ceton kuɗi* ba, domin yawancin matsalolin da aka yiwa alama ba za su taɓa zama abubuwan da suka faru ba (matsalar kaɗan na yanayin da ba a yi ba). Lissafa shi: jimillar kuɗin ƙofa a kowane lokaci da abubuwan da suka faru da aka kauce wa × kuɗin abu — tsarin misali ɗaya, tare da [NNT](../adadin-da-ake-bukatar-magani/) a matsayin naúrar kowane kama.

## Tarkuna

- **Da'awar ceton kuɗi lokacin da shaida ke goyon bayan tasirin kuɗi** — kuskuren da ke bayyana fafutukar rigakafi a fagage biyu.
- **Rage-ragen nan gaba ba tare da rage daraja ba**: fa'idodi shekaru 15 gaba a ƙimar fuska.
- **Watsi da kuɗin yawan ganewa/yawan magani**: rigakafi yana samun cuta ta ƙarya ma — duba [tattalin arzikin gwajin bincike](../tattalin-arzikin-gwajin-bincike/).
