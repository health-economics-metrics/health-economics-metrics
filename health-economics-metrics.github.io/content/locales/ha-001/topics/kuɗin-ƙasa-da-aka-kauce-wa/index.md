# Kuɗin Ƙasa da Aka Kauce Wa

Kuɗin ƙasa da aka kauce wa (ragewar kuɗi) kuɗaɗen magani na gaba ne da aka hana ta hanyar aiki da wuri ko mafi kyau, aka rage su daga kuɗin shiga tsakanin kansa. Ragewa ita ce hanyar da shiga tsakani zai iya zama *mai mamaya* — mai arha **kuma** mafi kyau — kuma su ne layin da aka fi ƙidayawa sau biyu, aka fi yin da'awa a kai a tattalin arzikin lafiya.

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

Kusan kowace shawarar daraja ta lafiyar dijital tana ɗauke da da'awar ragewa: "manhajarmu tana hana shigar asibiti," "faɗakarwarmu tana hana lalacewa," "dandamalinmu yana kauce wa gwaje-gwaje masu maimaitawa." Idan ragewar ta gaske ce, tana sauya tattalin arziki (duba misalin [ICER](../rabon-ƙarin-tasirin-kuɗi/), inda ragewa ta £600k ke kafa shari'ar). Masu biya sun san wannan — don haka da'awar ragewa tana jawo mafi tsananin bincike a kowane tantancewa. Ƙa'idodin sahihanci na ƙasa su ne ke raba samfurin da za a iya ba da kuɗi da tallace-tallace.

## Lissafin

```
Net cost = intervention cost − Σ offsets

A valid offset must be:
  Attributable — causally linked to the intervention (comparator evidence)
  Marginal     — the money actually stops being spent, at marginal not
                 average cost (see marginal-vs-average-cost.md)
  Probability- — weighted by P(the downstream event would have occurred)
  weighted
  Discounted   — future avoided costs at present value
  Unique       — counted once, in one benefit line
```

## Misali da aka lissafa

"Wannan da'awar haɗarin ƙaura, an yi ta daidai": manhajar sa ido kan raunuka ga marasa lafiya 5,000 bayan tiyata tana da'awar kauce wa komawa asibiti saboda kamuwa.

```
Baseline readmission for infection: 4.0% ; with app (RCT): 3.1%
Attributable events avoided = 5,000 × 0.009 = 45/year
Cost per readmission spell (marginal, this trust): £3,200
Offset = 45 × 3,200 = £144,000/year
App cost = 5,000 × £20 = £100,000/year
Net cost = −£44,000 → genuinely cost-saving, with:
  attribution from an RCT ✓  marginal costing ✓  probability from trial data ✓
```

Da'awa guda wadda aka gina a kan "komawa asibiti na kashe £5,800 a matsakaici, za mu hana da yawa" ta fadi dukan gwaje-gwaje huɗun kuma ta cancanci ƙin da take samu.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

"Wannan ƙaurar tana kauce wa sake rubutawa nan gaba" da'awar ragewa ce, kuma ƙa'idodin tattalin arzikin lafiya suna sa ta zama ta gaskiya:

- **Kuɗin abin da zai faru in ba haka ba**: nawa sake rubutawar zai kashe a zahiri, da wace shaida?
- **Yiwuwa**: yaya yiwuwar wannan nan gaba? (Ba 100% ba — ana kashe samfura, fifiko yana canzawa.)
- **Rage daraja**: sake rubutawa da aka kauce wa a shekara ta 4 a rage daraja 3.5–10% darajarsa ta fi ƙanƙanta da asalinta.
- **Keɓantacce**: kar a yi da'awar sake rubutawa iri ɗaya a layin bashin fasaha da layin riƙewa duka.

`Offset value = P(future event) × counterfactual cost × discount factor` — a rubuta wannan layi a cikin shawarar kuma a kalli ƙiyasin ya zama abin muhawara, wanda shi ne manufar.

## Tarkuna

- **Ƙidaya sau biyu** — shigar asibiti ɗaya da aka kauce wa an yi da'awarsa a matsayin ragewa, da kwanakin gado, da QALY da aka haɗa da kuɗi.
- **Ragewa bisa matsakaicin kuɗi** ga abubuwan da kuɗaɗensu tsayayyu suna ci gaba ko ta yaya.
- **Yiwuwar 100% a ɓoye** ga abubuwan ƙasa waɗanda kawai suka yiwu.
- **Ragewa ga wasu kasafin kuɗi** da aka gabatar a matsayin tanadi ga mai biyan da ake roƙo — duba [mahangar bincike](../mahangar-bincike/).
