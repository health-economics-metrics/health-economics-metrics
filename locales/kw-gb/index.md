# Metryks Ekonomek Yeghes

Rager-ger lanwes dhe vatematik ekonomek yeghes, ensamplow, ha rasonyans. Pub restren a dhisplegh unn metrik po kysyans: styrans, perag yth yw posek, an matematik, ensampel gwrys, an kolm gans ynjynnyans medhelweyth, fowtys, ha fontynnow.

Nowydh omma? Dallethewgh gans [kost an dewis](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/opportunity-cost/), [bledhen bewnans addasys-sawder](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/quality-adjusted-life-year/), ha [kost dalgh](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-of-delay/) — an try tybyans may ma pub tra aral ow drehevel warnodhans.

## Selyow rasonyans ekonomek

- [Kost an Dewis](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/opportunity-cost/) — an talvos a'n gwell alternativ gesys; perag y whra budjetow sevys pub dewisyans dasplas
- [Diskontya ha Preferans Termyn](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/discounting-and-time-preference/) — talvosow present, an ratyo 3.5% Green Book/NICE
- [Perspektiv Analys](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/analysis-perspective/) — paysyer erbynn provider erbynn sosyal: a biw kostow a gontyr
- [Horizon Termyn](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/time-horizon/) — pygemmys hys dhe gontya kostow hag efektow, ha gwari horizon
- [Kost Marginal erbynn Kres](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/marginal-vs-average-cost/) — perag na sav gwely rydhys y gost kres
- [Sawyansow Relasya Arghans erbynn Heb Relasya Arghans](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cash-releasing-vs-non-cash-releasing/) — an previ onester rag pub klaym "termyn sawys"
- [Analys Tebelder](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/sensitivity-analysis/) — diagramow tornado; pyth tybyans a dhon dha gas
- [Analys Ansylder Probabilistek (PSA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/probabilistic-sensitivity-analysis/) — Monte Carlo, CEACs, probabilita a vos ewn
- [Talvos Ragwelys a Wodhvos Perfydh (EVPI)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/expected-value-of-perfect-information/) — prisya an pilot kyns y rolya
- [Talvos Ragwelys a Sampel Kedhlow (EVSI)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/expected-value-of-sample-information/) — prisya studhya *spesyfek* arghyssys, na dileya oll an dowtyans
- [Gwerthjyans Opsyonow Gwir](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/real-options-valuation/) — prisya an opsyon dhe ystynna ragdres skwithys wosa, na an opsyon dhe gavos kedhlow kensa
- [Fordh Kapital Denel erbynn Fordh Kost Kesstryf](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/human-capital-and-friction-cost/) — dyw fordh dhe dalvosa produktivita kellys, dyffrans 2x+ yn kost derivys
- [Dominans ha Harth Effeythusnans](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/dominance-and-efficiency-frontier/) — dilea dewisyansow na dal dhe nebonan dhe'n dewis

## Musurow sewyans

- [Bledhen Bewnans Addasys-Anabalder (QALY)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/quality-adjusted-life-year/) — an arghans kemmyn a dalvos yeghes
- [Bledhen Bewnans Addasys-Anabalder (DALY)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/disability-adjusted-life-year/) — an gesunnans-bern, steren a-barth an bys; musur klevesow bys
- [EQ-5D](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/eq-5d/) — an ynstrument a-dhelergh an moyha a bwysyow utilita QALY
- [Dyskudhans Utilita Kenwerth-Termyn (TTO)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/time-trade-off-utility/) — fatell yw pwys utilita dyskudhys yn gwir dhort gorthyber
- [Ratyo Kost-Effeythusnans Ynkressys (ICER)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/incremental-cost-effectiveness-ratio/) — kost ekstra rag pub unys ekstra a yeghes
- [Harthow Bodh-dhe-Dhalleth](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/willingness-to-pay-thresholds/) — NICE £20–30k/QALY ha linennow erel an bys
- [Talvos Bewnans Statistek (VSL)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/value-of-a-statistical-life/) — an dhewis marhas-lavur dhe dalvosa herwydh harth
- [Prow Mona Nett (NMB)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/net-monetary-benefit/) — talvos le kost, gwrys yn ewn
- [Bledhydhyow Bewnans Gwynnys (LYG)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/life-years-gained/) — survyvans matematik, ha'n gwariyans ekwita evLYG
- [Gwaytyans Bewnans Addasys-Yeghes (HALE)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/health-adjusted-life-expectancy/) — rolya oll-bobel a vledhynyow yagh
- [Diffyk QALY ha Chanjyoryon Ankevri](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/qaly-shortfall-and-severity-modifiers/) — perag QALYs a bobel klavha a dal moy
- [Lettyans Produktivita Ober ha Aktivita (WPAI)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/work-productivity-and-activity-impairment/) — absens erbynn presens-lettys, hanter kudhys a'n kost

## Spesyow analys ekonomek

- [Dehweles dhe Investyans (ROI)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/return-on-investment/) — an metrik rannys, gans parametrys derivys
- [Dehweles Sosyal dhe Investyans (SROI)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/social-return-on-investment/) — talvosa an pyth na wra marhasow prisya
- [Keskomparyans ICER Kroes-Arghans](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cross-currency-icer-comparison/) — PPP erbynn FX marhas; an dewisyans treylya a yll treylya dewisyans degemeres

## Ynjynnyans medhelweyth ha delivrans digidel

- [Metryks Lanwes](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/flow-metrics/) — Lagh Little, WIP, effeythusnans lanwes; matematik rew kesys a glavjiyow ha pibrennow
- [WSJF ha CD3](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/wsjf-and-cd3/) — prederyans dewder-talvos; an backlog avoel tabel-lyga QALY
- [SPACE ha DevEx](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/space-and-devex/) — produktivita liesdimensyon; an dyskas EQ-5D rag metryks ynjynnyans
- [Dalgh Teknegel](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/technical-debt/) — pennsom, dheweth, ha ekonomek kleves-kronek rag kodhvow
- [Kost Perghenogeth Hollow (TCO)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/total-cost-of-ownership/) — synsyans yw 50–80%; fowt pris-medhegieth naïv yn medhelweyth
- [Ekonomek Unys Skeul (FinOps)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cloud-unit-economics/) — kost rag unys a sewyans; kost daspar an servis digidel
- [Dyssehtyans Kost Ewn-Kans](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/exact-cents-cost-allocation/) — an fordh moyha-restyans; dasrann hollow may sommyans dhe'n dhewis
- [Kuntell Kost Sawys-Arghans](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/currency-safe-cost-rollup/) — `Money` dekvedh-ewn, na `f64`, rag hollowyow a res dasyunna dhe'n peni
- [Gwil erbynn Prena](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/build-vs-buy/) — keskomparyans risk-addasys gans an term dalgh prisys
- [Kowlwrians Prowyow](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/benefits-realization/) — awdytya bos prow ragwelys hwarvedhys yn gwir
- [Metryks Servis GDS](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/gds-service-metrics/) — kost rag treuswerth, plegadow, kowlwrans, kemmeres
