How to ask a question about Health Economics Metrics

This is the Q&A category for Health Economics Metrics. Questions here can be marked with an accepted answer, so later readers can find the solution quickly.

## Before you ask

- Search existing discussions and the book at https://health-economics-metrics.github.io/health-economics-metrics/.
- Use Issues (https://github.com/health-economics-metrics/health-economics-metrics/issues) instead for errors, broken links, or typos.

## What to include

- **Goal:** what you are trying to do, in one sentence.
- **What I tried:** the steps you took, in order.
- **Expected and actual:** what you hoped for and what happened. Use the exact wording of any message.
- **Context:** which topic or page of the book you are using, and your role or setting if it matters.
- **Minimal example:** the smallest case that shows the problem, as copyable text rather than a screenshot.

## Good habits

- Write a specific title that names the problem, not "Help".
- Ask one question per discussion.
- Remove secrets and personal data.
- Mark the reply that answered your question, and thank the people who helped.
