Welcome to Health Economics Metrics Discussions

Health Economics Metrics now has GitHub Discussions, a place to ask questions, suggest ideas, and share what you have done with it.

Health Economics Metrics is an introduction to health economics math, examples, and reasoning, where each topic covers one metric or concept with a definition, the math, a worked example, pitfalls, and sources. It is written for software engineers and others who need to reason about health economics.

## What this means for you

- You can ask questions and get answers from other readers.
- You can suggest topics, fixes, and improvements before anyone writes them.
- You can share how you use the book.

## Where to go for what

- Questions about using the book: Q&A
- Suggestions for new or changed topics: Ideas
- Quick community votes: Polls
- Things you have built or tried with the book: Show and tell
- Everything else: General
- Errors, broken links, or typos: Issues (https://github.com/health-economics-metrics/health-economics-metrics/issues)
