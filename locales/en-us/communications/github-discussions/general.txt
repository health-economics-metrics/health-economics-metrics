Say hello and tell us how you use Health Economics Metrics

Welcome to the Health Economics Metrics Discussions. This is the place for introductions, opinions, and open conversation about the book.

I am a maintainer of the project. I wrote Health Economics Metrics because I wanted an introduction to health economics math, examples, and reasoning, where each topic covers one metric or concept with a definition, the math, a worked example, pitfalls, and sources to be easy to find and use. I would like to hear who is reading it and why.

**One question to start:** Which health economics metric or concept would you like to see explained with a worked example?

A sentence or two is plenty. Replies to other people are welcome too.

## Where other topics belong

- Suggestions for new or changed topics: Ideas
- Errors or broken links: Issues (https://github.com/health-economics-metrics/health-economics-metrics/issues)
