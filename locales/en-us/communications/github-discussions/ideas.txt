How to suggest an idea for Health Economics Metrics

Have an idea for Health Economics Metrics? This is the place to propose a new topic, a change to an existing one, or a better way to explain something. One idea per discussion keeps feedback easy to follow.

## How to suggest an idea

Copy this template into a new discussion in Ideas:

**Title:** the outcome or the problem, not the solution. For example, "Explain a metric or concept such as opportunity cost, quality-adjusted life years, or cost of delay".

**Problem:** What is missing, wrong, or hard to understand? Who is affected, and when?

**Proposal:** What would you add or change? A short list is fine.

**Alternatives:** What do readers do today? Is doing nothing acceptable?

**Costs and trade-offs:** Could this make the book longer, harder to maintain, or less focused?

**Open questions:** What would you like feedback on?

## What happens next

Maintainers read new ideas and reply in the discussion. An idea is not promised a place in the book. If an idea is agreed, it may become an issue (https://github.com/health-economics-metrics/health-economics-metrics/issues) so the work can be tracked.

Errors and typos belong in Issues, and questions belong in Q&A.
