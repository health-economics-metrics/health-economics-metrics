# Metrik Ekonomi Kesehatan

Pambuka lengkap babagan matematika, conto, lan nalar ekonomi kesehatan. Saben berkas mbahas siji metrik utawa konsep: definisi, kenapa penting, matematikane, conto sing diitung, sambungane karo rékayasa piranti lunak, jebakan, lan sumber.

Anyar ing kene? Wiwiti karo [biaya peluang](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/opportunity-cost/), [taun urip sing disesuaikan kualitas](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/quality-adjusted-life-year/), lan [biaya keterlambatan](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-of-delay/) — telung gagasan sing didadekake dhasar kabeh liyane.

## Dasar-dasar nalar ekonomi

- [Biaya Peluang](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/opportunity-cost/) — nilai alternatif paling apik sing dilepasake; kenapa anggaran tetep ndadekake saben pilihan dadi pamindahan
- [Diskon lan Preferensi Wektu](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/discounting-and-time-preference/) — nilai saiki, tarif 3,5% Green Book/NICE
- [Perspektif Analisis](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/analysis-perspective/) — pambayar vs panyedhia vs masarakat: biaya sapa sing diétung
- [Cakrawala Wektu](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/time-horizon/) — suwene ngétung biaya lan efek, lan dolanan cakrawala
- [Biaya Marginal vs Rata-rata](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/marginal-vs-average-cost/) — kenapa ngosongake ranjang ora ngirit biaya rata-ratane
- [Penghematan Cash-Releasing vs Non-Cash-Releasing](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cash-releasing-vs-non-cash-releasing/) — tes kajujuran kanggo saben klaim "wektu sing dihemat"
- [Analisis Sensitivitas](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/sensitivity-analysis/) — diagram tornado; asumsi endi sing nyangga kasus panjenengan
- [Analisis Sensitivitas Probabilistik (PSA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/probabilistic-sensitivity-analysis/) — Monte Carlo, CEAC, kemungkinan bener
- [Nilai Informasi Sampurna sing Dikarepake (EVPI)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/expected-value-of-perfect-information/) — nemtokake rega pilot sadurunge dilakoni
- [Nilai Informasi Sampel sing Dikarepake (EVSI)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/expected-value-of-sample-information/) — nemtokake rega *siji* studi sing diusulake, dudu ngilangi kabeh ketidakpastian
- [Valuasi Opsi Nyata](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/real-options-valuation/) — nemtokake rega opsi kanggo nggedhekake proyek bertahap mengko, dudu opsi nglumpukake informasi dhisik
- [Pendekatan Modal Manungsa vs Metode Biaya Gesekan](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/human-capital-and-friction-cost/) — rong cara ngregani produktivitas sing ilang, bedane biaya sing dilaporake 2x+
- [Dominansi lan Wates Efisiensi](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/dominance-and-efficiency-frontier/) — ngilangi opsi sing ora kudu dipilih sapa wae

## Ukuran asil

- [Taun Urip sing Disesuaikan Kualitas (QALY)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/quality-adjusted-life-year/) — mata uang umum nilai kesehatan
- [Taun Urip Sing Disesuaikan Disabilitas (DALY)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/disability-adjusted-life-year/) — pasangan sisih beban; metrik kesehatan global
- [EQ-5D](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/eq-5d/) — instrumen ing mburine umume bobot utilitas QALY
- [Elisitasi Utilitas Time Trade-Off (TTO)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/time-trade-off-utility/) — carane bobot utilitas bener-bener dijupuk saka responden
- [Rasio Efektivitas-Biaya Inkremental (ICER)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/incremental-cost-effectiveness-ratio/) — biaya tambahan saben unit kesehatan tambahan
- [Ambang Kesediaan-Mbayar](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/willingness-to-pay-thresholds/) — NICE £20–30k/QALY lan garis liyane ing donya
- [Nilai Nyawa Statistik (VSL)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/value-of-a-statistical-life/) — alternatif pasar tenaga kerja tinimbang penilaian adhedhasar ambang
- [Manfaat Moneter Net (NMB)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/net-monetary-benefit/) — nilai dikurangi biaya, dilakoni kanthi bener
- [Taun Urip sing Dipikolehi (LYG)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/life-years-gained/) — etungan kaslametan, lan varian keadilan evLYG
- [Pangarep-arep Urip Sing Disesuaikan Kesehatan (HALE)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/health-adjusted-life-expectancy/) — akuntansi taun sehat tingkat populasi
- [Shortfall QALY lan Pengubah Keparahan](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/qaly-shortfall-and-severity-modifiers/) — kenapa QALY populasi sing luwih lara diétung luwih akèh
- [Gangguan Produktivitas Kerja lan Aktivitas (WPAI)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/work-productivity-and-activity-impairment/) — absenteisme vs presenteisme, separo biaya sing didhelikake

## Jinis analisis ekonomi

- [Analisis Efektivitas-Biaya (CEA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-effectiveness-analysis/) — biaya saben unit asil alami
- [Analisis Biaya-Utilitas (CUA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-utility-analysis/) — biaya saben QALY; mbandhingake intervensi sing beda
- [Analisis Biaya-Manfaat (CBA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-benefit-analysis/) — kabeh ing wujud dhuwit; NPV Green Book
- [Analisis Minimalisasi Biaya (CMA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-minimization-analysis/) — opsi paling murah, sawise mbuktekake kesetaraan
- [Analisis Biaya-Akibat (CCA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-consequence-analysis/) — tabel sing dipérang-pérang; pilihan NICE kanggo kesehatan digital
- [Analisis Dampak Anggaran (BIA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/budget-impact-analysis/) — kamampuan mbayar, béda saka nilai
- [Return on Investment (ROI)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/return-on-investment/) — metrik bareng, kanthi parameter sing diumumake
- [Return on Investment Sosial (SROI)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/social-return-on-investment/) — ngowahi dadi dhuwit apa sing ora diregani pasar
- [Perbandingan ICER Lintas Mata Uang](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cross-currency-icer-comparison/) — PPP vs FX pasar; pilihan konversi sing bisa mbalikake kaputusan adopsi

## Kerangka HTA lan ekonomi pencegahan

- [Penilaian Teknologi Kesehatan (HTA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/health-technology-assessment/) — NICE, ICER (AS), CADTH: sapa sing mutusake apa sing pantes dituku
- [Simulasi Kohort Markov](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/markov-cohort-simulation/) — carane model HTA multi-siklus bener-bener disimulasi kohort-demi-siklus
- [Kerangka Standar Bukti NICE (ESF)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/nice-evidence-standards-framework/) — syarat bukti adhedhasar tingkat resiko kanggo kesehatan digital
- [Jalur Cepet DiGA Jerman](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/diga-fast-track/) — aplikasi kanthi resep; daftar sementara kanthi wates wektu bukti
- [Jumlah sing Perlu Diobati (NNT)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/number-needed-to-treat/) — unit upaya-saben-manfaat sing njaga klaim tetep jujur
- [Fraksi Atribut Populasi (PAF)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/population-attributable-fraction/) — pira beban penyakit sing pancen pantes dikejar saka faktor resiko
- [Ekonomi Pencegahan](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/prevention-economics/) — kenapa pencegahan hemat-biaya nanging arang ngirit dhuwit
- [Ekonomi Skrining](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/screening-economics/) — Wilson–Jungner, runtuhe PPV ing prevalensi murah, kesel waspada
- [Jumlah sing Perlu Diskrining (NNS)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/number-needed-to-screen/) — analog NNT ing tingkat program skrining
- [Biaya Hilir sing Dihindari](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/avoided-downstream-costs/) — pangimbang biaya lan aturan sing ndadekake kredibel
- [Analisis Keputusan Multi-Kriteria (MCDA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/multi-criteria-decision-analysis/) — skoring bobot nalika siji ambang ora cukup
- [Jejak Karbon saben QALY](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/carbon-footprint-per-qaly/) — komitmen net-zero NHS ketemu biaya-saben-QALY

## Rékayasa piranti lunak lan pangiriman digital

- [Biaya Keterlambatan (CoD)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-of-delay/) — £/minggu utawa QALY/minggu saka ora dikirim; metrik jembatan utama
- [Metrik DORA](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/dora-metrics/) — kinerja pangiriman, diterjemahake dadi istilah ekonomi kesehatan
- [Metrik Aliran](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/flow-metrics/) — Hukum Little, WIP, efisiensi aliran; matematika antrian bareng rumah sakit lan pipeline
- [WSJF lan CD3](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/wsjf-and-cd3/) — prioritisasi kapadhetan nilai; backlog minangka tabel liga QALY
- [SPACE lan DevEx](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/space-and-devex/) — produktivitas multi-dimensi; piwulang EQ-5D kanggo metrik rékayasa
- [Utang Teknis](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/technical-debt/) — pokok, bunga, lan ekonomi penyakit kronis kanggo basis kode
- [Total Biaya Kepemilikan (TCO)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/total-cost-of-ownership/) — pangopènan iku 50–80%; kesalahan rega-obat naif ing piranti lunak
- [Ekonomi Unit Cloud (FinOps)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cloud-unit-economics/) — biaya saben unit output; biaya rujukan layanan digital
- [Alokasi Biaya Sen-Pas](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/exact-cents-cost-allocation/) — metode sisa-panggedhe; mbagi total supaya bagiane bali dadi jumlah persis
- [Rollup Biaya Aman-Mata-Uang](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/currency-safe-cost-rollup/) — `Money` desimal-pas, dudu `f64`, kanggo total sing kudu cocog nganti sen
- [Mbangun vs Tuku](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/build-vs-buy/) — mbandhingake resiko-disesuaikan kanthi istilah tundha direga
- [Realisasi Manfaat](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/benefits-realization/) — ngaudit manawa manfaat ramalan bener-bener kedadeyan
- [Metrik Layanan GDS](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/gds-service-metrics/) — biaya saben transaksi, kepuasan, rampung, panampa
