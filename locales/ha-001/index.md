# Ma'aunin Tattalin Arzikin Lafiya

Cikakken gabatarwa ga lissafi, misalai, da dalilai na tattalin arzikin lafiya. Kowane fayil yana kunshe da ma'auni ko manufa guda ɗaya: ma'ana, dalilin muhimmancinta, lissafin, misali da aka lissafa, alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja, tarkuna, da tushe.

Sabo ne a nan? Fara da [Farashin Damar da Aka Bari](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/opportunity-cost/), [Shekarar Rayuwa da Aka Daidaita da Inganci (QALY)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/quality-adjusted-life-year/), da [Kuɗin Jinkiri](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-of-delay/) — manufofi uku da duk sauran abubuwa suke ginuwa a kansu.

## Tushen tunanin tattalin arziki

- [Farashin Damar da Aka Bari](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/opportunity-cost/) — darajar mafi kyawun zaɓin da aka bari; dalilin da ya sa kasafin kuɗi mai tsayayye ke mayar da kowane zaɓi hijirar kuɗi
- [Rage Daraja da Fifikon Lokaci](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/discounting-and-time-preference/) — darajojin yanzu, kashi 3.5% na Green Book/NICE
- [Mahangar Bincike](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/analysis-perspective/) — mai biya da mai bayarwa da al'umma: kuɗin wane ne ake ƙidaya
- [Tsawon Lokacin Bincike](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/time-horizon/) — tsawon lokacin da za a ƙidaya kuɗi da sakamako, da yaudarar tsawon lokaci
- [Kuɗin Ƙari da Matsakaicin Kuɗi](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/marginal-vs-average-cost/) — dalilin da ya sa sakin gado ba ya ceton matsakaicin kuɗinsa
- [Tanadi Mai Sakin Kuɗi da Wanda Ba Ya Sakin Kuɗi](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cash-releasing-vs-non-cash-releasing/) — gwajin gaskiya ga kowace da'awar "lokacin da aka ceta"
- [Binciken Jin Canji](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/sensitivity-analysis/) — zanen guguwa; wace zato ce ke ɗauke da hujjarka
- [Binciken Jin Canji na Yiwuwa](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/probabilistic-sensitivity-analysis/) — Monte Carlo, CEAC, yiwuwar kasancewa daidai
- [Darajar da Ake Tsammani ta Cikakken Bayani](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/expected-value-of-perfect-information/) — ƙayyade farashin gwajin kafin a gudanar da shi
- [Darajar da Ake Tsammani ta Bayanin Samfuri (EVSI)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/expected-value-of-sample-information/) — ƙayyade farashin takamaiman binciken da aka gabatar, ba kawar da dukkan rashin tabbas ba
- [Kimanta Zaɓuɓɓuka na Gaske](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/real-options-valuation/) — ƙayyade farashin zaɓin faɗaɗa aikin matakai daga baya, maimakon zaɓin tattara bayanai tukuna
- [Tsarin Jarin Ɗan Adam da Hanyar Kuɗin Gogayya](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/human-capital-and-friction-cost/) — hanyoyi biyu na kimanta asarar aiki, bambanci sama da ninki 2 a kuɗin da aka bayar da rahoto
- [Mamaya da Iyakar Inganci](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/dominance-and-efficiency-frontier/) — cire zaɓuɓɓukan da bai kamata kowa ya zaɓa ba

## Ma'aunan sakamako

- [Shekarar Rayuwa da Aka Daidaita da Inganci (QALY)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/quality-adjusted-life-year/) — kuɗin tafiyar da darajar lafiya a tsakanin kowa
- [Shekarar Rayuwa da Aka Daidaita da Nakasa (DALY)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/disability-adjusted-life-year/) — madubin ɓangaren nauyi; ma'aunin lafiyar duniya
- [EQ-5D](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/eq-5d/) — kayan aikin da ke bayan yawancin ma'aunan amfani na QALY
- [Fitar da Amfani ta Musayar Lokaci (TTO)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/time-trade-off-utility/) — yadda ake fitar da ma'aunin amfani da gaske daga mai amsawa
- [Rabon Ƙarin Tasirin Kuɗi (ICER)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/incremental-cost-effectiveness-ratio/) — ƙarin kuɗi ga kowace ƙarin naúrar lafiya
- [Iyakokin Shirin Biya](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/willingness-to-pay-thresholds/) — £20–30k/QALY na NICE da sauran layukan duniya
- [Darajar Rayuwar Ƙididdiga (VSL)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/value-of-a-statistical-life/) — madadin kasuwar ƙwadago ga kimanta bisa iyaka
- [Ribar Kuɗi Net (NMB)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/net-monetary-benefit/) — daraja ragewa kuɗi, a yi daidai
- [Shekarun Rayuwa da Aka Samu](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/life-years-gained/) — lissafin rayuwa, da bambancin daidaito evLYG
- [Tsawon Rai Mai Lafiya (HALE)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/health-adjusted-life-expectancy/) — lissafin shekaru masu lafiya a matakin al'umma
- [Gibin QALY da Masu Gyaran Tsanani](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/qaly-shortfall-and-severity-modifiers/) — dalilin da ya sa QALY na al'ummomi marasa lafiya ke da nauyi
- [Tawaya a Aiki da Ayyuka (WPAI)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/work-productivity-and-activity-impairment/) — rashin zuwa aiki da zuwa aiki ba da lafiya, ɓoyayyen rabin kuɗin

## Nau'o'in binciken tattalin arziki

- [Binciken Tasirin Kuɗi (CEA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-effectiveness-analysis/) — kuɗi ga kowace naúrar sakamako ta halitta
- [Binciken Amfanin Kuɗi (CUA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-utility-analysis/) — kuɗi ga kowane QALY; kwatanta ayyuka daban-daban
- [Binciken Riba da Kuɗi (CBA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-benefit-analysis/) — komai a cikin kuɗi; NPV na Green Book
- [Binciken Rage Kuɗi (CMA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-minimization-analysis/) — zaɓi mafi arha, bayan tabbatar da daidaito
- [Binciken Sakamakon Kuɗi (CCA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-consequence-analysis/) — tebur mai rarrabe; fifikon NICE ga lafiyar dijital
- [Binciken Tasirin Kasafin Kuɗi (BIA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/budget-impact-analysis/) — iya biya, wanda ya bambanta da daraja
- [Dawowar Jari (ROI)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/return-on-investment/) — ma'aunin da kowa ke amfani da shi, tare da sigogi da aka bayyana
- [Dawowar Jari ta Zamantakewa (SROI)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/social-return-on-investment/) — mayar da abin da kasuwa ba ta farashi zuwa kuɗi
- [Kwatanta ICER Tsakanin Kuɗaɗe](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cross-currency-icer-comparison/) — PPP da canjin kuɗin kasuwa; zaɓin sauyi wanda zai iya juya shawarar karɓa

## Tattalin arzikin aikin tsarin lafiya

- [Kwanakin Gado da Aka Ceta](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/bed-days-saved/) — babbar fa'ida, da tarkunan kimanta ta
- [Tsawon Zaman Asibiti](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/length-of-stay/) — lokacin zagayen asibiti
- [Adadin Komawa Asibiti](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/readmission-rate/) — adadin gazawar sauyi na tsarin lafiya
- [Adadin Rashin Halartar Alƙawari (DNA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/did-not-attend-rate/) — alƙawuran da aka rasa; mafi tsantsar ma'aunin ɓarna
- [Kauce wa Zuwa Gaggawa](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/emergency-attendance-avoidance/) — tattalin arzikin shiga tsakani a farkon lokaci
- [Farashin Ƙasa da Kuɗin Naúra](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/national-tariff-and-unit-costs/) — littafin farashin NHS da tsarin ƙididdige kuɗi
- [Daga Turawa zuwa Magani (RTT)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/referral-to-treatment/) — ƙa'idar makonni 18 a matsayin ma'aunin lokacin jira
- [Tasirin Jerin Jira](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/waiting-list-impact/) — mayar da sa'o'in da aka ceta zuwa marasa lafiya da aka gani
- [Lokacin Ma'aikaci](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/practitioner-time/) — kimanta ƙarfin da ke zama ƙugiya, ba albashi ba
- [Riƙe Ma'aikata](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/workforce-retention/) — kuɗin sauyin ma'aikata da tattalin arzikin gajiya
- [Kuɗin Fitar da Aiki Waje da Za a Iya Kauce Wa](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/avoidable-outsourcing-costs/) — dawo da aiki mai tsada gida
- [Inganta Albarkatu na Ƙasa](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/downstream-resource-optimization/) — buɗe aikin da kowa ke jira
- [Shiga Tsakani da Wuri](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/earlier-intervention/) — tattalin arzikin yin magani kafin cuta ta ci gaba
- [Ƙarfin Samar da Daraja (juyin aiki)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/value-generating-capacity-operational-turnaround/) — samar da ƙarfi ba tare da ɗaukar sabbin ma'aikata ba
- [Tanadin Kuɗi Mai Tsauri Mai Sakin Kuɗi (kare gibi)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/hard-cash-releasing-savings-deficit-defence/) — share layukan kasafin kuɗi; ma'aunin CFO

## Tsare-tsaren HTA da tattalin arzikin rigakafi

- [Tantance Fasahar Lafiya (HTA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/health-technology-assessment/) — NICE, ICER (Amurka), CADTH: wane ne ke yanke abin da ya cancanci saye
- [Kwaikwayon Ƙungiya na Markov](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/markov-cohort-simulation/) — yadda ake kwaikwayon samfurin HTA mai zagaye da yawa ƙungiya bayan ƙungiya, zagaye bayan zagaye
- [Tsarin Matsayin Shaida na NICE](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/nice-evidence-standards-framework/) — buƙatun shaida bisa matakan haɗari ga lafiyar dijital
- [Hanyar Gaggawa ta DiGA ta Jamus](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/diga-fast-track/) — manhajoji da takardar magani; jerin wucin gadi tare da wa'adin shaida
- [Adadin da Ake Bukatar Magani (NNT)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/number-needed-to-treat/) — naúrorin ƙoƙari-ga-fa'ida da ke tabbatar da gaskiyar da'awa
- [Kaso Mai Alaƙa da Jama'a (PAF)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/population-attributable-fraction/) — nauyin cuta nawa abin haɗari ya cancanci a bi
- [Tattalin Arzikin Rigakafi](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/prevention-economics/) — dalilin da ya sa rigakafi ke da tasirin kuɗi amma da wuya ya ceci kuɗi
- [Tattalin Arzikin Gwajin Bincike](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/screening-economics/) — Wilson–Jungner, faɗuwar PPV a ƙarancin yaɗuwa, gajiyar faɗakarwa
- [Adadin da Ake Bukatar Bincike (NNS)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/number-needed-to-screen/) — takwaran NNT a matakin shirin binciken
- [Kuɗin Ƙasa da Aka Kauce Wa](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/avoided-downstream-costs/) — rage kuɗi da ƙa'idojin da ke sa su zama abin dogaro
- [Binciken Shawara Mai Ma'auni Da Yawa (MCDA)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/multi-criteria-decision-analysis/) — ba da maki mai nauyi lokacin da iyaka ɗaya bai isa ba
- [Sawun Carbon ga Kowane QALY](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/carbon-footprint-per-qaly/) — alƙawarin NHS na sifili-net ya haɗu da kuɗi-ga-QALY

## Injiniyancin manhaja da isar da dijital

- [Kuɗin Jinkiri](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cost-of-delay/) — £/mako ko QALY/mako na rashin isarwa; babban ma'aunin gada
- [Ma'aunan DORA](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/dora-metrics/) — aikin isarwa, an fassara shi zuwa harshen tattalin arzikin lafiya
- [Ma'aunan Gudana](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/flow-metrics/) — Dokar Little, WIP, ingancin gudana; lissafin jerin gwano da asibitoci da bututun aiki ke rabawa
- [WSJF da CD3](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/wsjf-and-cd3/) — fifiko bisa yawan daraja; jerin aiki a matsayin teburin gasar QALY
- [SPACE da DevEx](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/space-and-devex/) — ƙwazo mai fuskoki da yawa; darasin EQ-5D ga ma'aunan injiniya
- [Bashin Fasaha](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/technical-debt/) — jari, riba, da tattalin arzikin cututtuka na dindindin ga lambobin manhaja
- [Jimillar Kuɗin Mallaka (TCO)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/total-cost-of-ownership/) — kulawa shi ne 50–80%; kuskuren farashin magani maras zurfi a manhaja
- [Tattalin Arzikin Naúrar Gajimare (FinOps)](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/cloud-unit-economics/) — kuɗi ga kowace naúrar fitarwa; kuɗin tunani na sabis na dijital
- [Raba Kuɗi Daidai Zuwa Kobo](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/exact-cents-cost-allocation/) — hanyar mafi girman saura; raba jimilla ta yadda sassan za su koma daidai
- [Jimlar Kuɗi Mai Tsaron Kuɗin Ƙasa](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/currency-safe-cost-rollup/) — `Money` mai ƙididdiga daidai, ba `f64` ba, ga jimloli da dole su daidaita zuwa kobo
- [Gina da Saye](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/build-vs-buy/) — kwatanta da aka daidaita da haɗari tare da lokacin jinkiri da aka yi wa farashi
- [Gane Fa'idodi](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/benefits-realization/) — tantance cewa fa'idodin da aka hasashe sun faru da gaske
- [Ma'aunan Sabis na GDS](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/gds-service-metrics/) — kuɗi ga kowane ciniki, gamsuwa, kammalawa, karɓuwa

## Gaggauta AI

- [Ƙwazon Mai Haɓaka Manhaja da AI](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/ai-developer-productivity/) — RCT na Copilot da RCT na METR; tasirin gwaji da tasirin amfani na gaske
- [Dawowar Jari ta AI](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/ai-return-on-investment/) — binciken da ya gano kashi 95% ba su samu dawowa ba da abin da kashi 5% suka yi daban
- [Tattalin Arzikin Naúrar Hasashe](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/inference-unit-economics/) — kuɗi ga kowane token, da ƙirar faɗuwar farashi maras ƙarewa
- [Ma'aunan Ingancin AI](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/ai-quality-metrics/) — adadin ƙirƙirar ƙarya a matsayin adadin cuta mai farashi
- [Tantance AI na Asibiti](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/clinical-ai-evaluation/) — ji, keɓancewa, AUROC, da dalilin da ya sa yaɗuwa ke mulkin tattalin arziki
- [Tantance AI ta Hukumomin Ƙa'ida](locales/en-gb-oxendict/topics/ai-regulatory-evaluation/) — FDA SaMD, PCCP, da tattalin arzikin sabunta samfura

## Sabuntawar ma'auni

Yawancin alkaluman da aka ambata suna sabuntuwa a kowace shekara (kuɗin naúrar NHS, farashin tsarin biyan kuɗi, gungun DORA, adadin DiGA, farashin LLM). Kowane daftari yana bayyana ranar ma'aunansa a cikin rubutu; a sake tabbatarwa kafin amfani da su a cikin shari'ar kasuwanci ta zahiri.
