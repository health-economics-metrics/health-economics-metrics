# Kwanakin Gado da Aka Ceta

Ranar gado ita ce majiyyaci ɗaya yana mamaye gadon asibiti ɗaya na tsawon rana ɗaya. "Kwanakin gado da aka ceta" — ta hanyar sallama da wuri, kaucewa shigar asibiti, ko ɗakunan marasa lafiya na zahiri — shi ne babbar fa'ida ta shari'o'in kasuwanci na dijital na NHS, kuma wadda aka fi ƙara darajarta fiye da kima.

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

Gadaje su ne ƙuntatawa mai ɗaure na kulawar gaggawa: idan gadaje suka cika, ana soke tiyatar zaɓi, motocin ɗaukar marasa lafiya suna jira a layi, kuma sashen gaggawa yana cunkoso. Shiga tsakani da ke 'yantar da kwanakin gado don haka yana ɗauke da daraja ta gaske — amma *irin* darajar ya dogara gaba ɗaya a kan abin da ke faruwa ga gadon da aka 'yantar. Daraktocin kuɗi sun koyi rage da'awar kwanakin gado marasa zurfi sosai; yin wannan lissafi daidai gwajin sahihanci ne.

## Lissafin

```
Bed days saved = patients affected × Δ length of stay (or admissions avoided × avg LOS)

Value depends on the use of the freed capacity:
  refilled with elective activity → value = activity income or waiting-list benefit
  ward closed / flexed down      → value = staffing + running cost released (cash)
  absorbed as slack              → value ≈ marginal (hotel) cost only, £50–£150/day
```

Matsakaicin cikakken kuɗin ranar gadon gaggawa ana ambatonsa sau da yawa a £400+ (National Cost Collection a tarihi ~£350 ga ƙarin kwanakin gado) — amma duba [kuɗin ƙari da matsakaicin kuɗi](../kuɗin-ƙari-da-matsakaicin-kuɗi/): matsakaici kusan ba shi ne tanadin ba.

## Misali da aka lissafa

"Ɗakin marasa lafiya na zahiri" na sa ido daga nesa yana bari marasa lafiya 600 a shekara su tafi gida kwana 2 da wuri: kwanakin gado 1,200 da aka ceta.

- **Da'awa maras zurfi**: 1,200 × £400 = £480,000. Ba daidai ba sai dai idan ɗaki ya rufe.
- **Da'awa ta gaskiya**: asusun yana cike gadajen da marasa lafiya na tiyatar ƙashi ta zaɓi. Kwanakin gado 1,200 ÷ matsakaicin zama kwana 3 = ƙarin zaman zaɓi 400 a ~£6,000 na kuɗin shiga kowanne a ƙarƙashin biyan bisa aiki = **£2.4M na ƙarin aikin da aka ba da kuɗi** (ragewa kuɗin ƙari na kula da waɗannan marasa lafiya), *tare da* marasa lafiya 400 da suka fita daga jerin jira. Kuɗin gudanar da ɗakin na zahiri (£350,000) yana raguwa daga wannan.

Ƙarfin da aka 'yantar wanda aka *sake amfani da shi* sau da yawa ya fi darajar da'awar kuɗi maras zurfi — amma nau'in daraja ne dabam kuma dole a yi masa lakabi haka ([mai sakin kuɗi da wanda ba ya sakin kuɗi](../tanadi-mai-sakin-kuɗi-da-wanda-ba-ya-sakin-kuɗi/)).

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

"Kwanakin sabar da aka ceta" yana aiki iri ɗaya. Rufe muhallin da ke kunne koyaushe yana sakin kuɗi ne kawai idan an dakatar da misalai ko ajiyar wuri ta ƙare; ƙarfin da aka mayar cikin tafkin yana da darajar kuɗin ƙarinsa (~0 a kan kashe-kashen da aka ɗaure). Horon da ya yi daidai: ga kowane tanadin da aka yi da'awa, a ambaci *hanyar* — an dakatar, an sake cika da aiki mai daraja, ko ya ƙafe. Manhajar da ke rage tsawon zaman asibiti (haɗa sallama, sa ido daga nesa, saurin sakamakon bincike) ya kamata ta yi samfurin dukkan yanayi uku kuma ta bar asusun ya zaɓa ga kowane ɗaki.

## Tarkuna

- **Ƙimanta ƙarfin ƙari da matsakaicin kuɗi** — kuskuren da aka fi sani.
- **Ƙidaya sau biyu**: kwanakin gado da aka ceta *da* shigar asibiti da aka kauce wa *da* rage jerin jira daga gado ɗaya da aka 'yantar.
- **Zaton kwanakin da aka ceta su ne masu tsada**: kwanakin da aka ceta a ƙarshen zama su ne mafi arha (ƙarancin tsananin rashin lafiya).
