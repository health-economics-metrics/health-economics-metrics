# Ƙarfin Samar da Daraja (juyin aiki)

Ƙarfin samar da daraja shi ne "fa'idar dama" ta lokacin da aka 'yanta: abin da asibiti zai iya *cimmawa* yanzu da sa'o'in da manhajarka ta saki. Wannan shi ne ma'aunin da ya fi muhimmanci ga Manyan Jami'an Aiki da Daraktocin Lafiya, domin yana magana da kuɗin da ake gudanar da su a kai — aiki, maƙasudai, da juyin aiki.

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

NHS tana fuskantar manyan jerin jira na turawa zuwa magani, kuma asibitocin da suka kasa cimma ƙa'idodin lokacin jira na ƙasa suna fuskantar bincike na ƙa'ida da shiga tsakani (duba [daga turawa zuwa magani](../daga-turawa-zuwa-magani/)). Daukar ma'aikata yana da jinkiri da ƙuntatawa; gine-gine tsayayyu ne. Hanya mai sauri kawai ita ce samun ƙarin aiki mai samar da daraja daga ma'aikata da sarari da ake da su. Manhajar da ke dawo da lokacin ƙwararru ba kawai "tana ceton kuɗi" ba — tana *ƙera ƙarfi*: asibitoci da ba za su wanzu ba, tantancewa da ba za a iya tsarawa ba, ba tare da daukar ma'aikata ko gini ba.

## Lissafin

```
Hidden capacity created = time released → activity units enabled × scheme value

Activity units: outpatient attendances, pre-op assessments, monitoring reviews
Scheme value:   national tariff / NHS Payment Scheme prices
                (see national-tariff-and-unit-costs.md)
```

Wannan shi ne kimantawar tushen fitarwa na [lokacin ma'aikaci](../lokacin-ma-aikaci/), an daidaita zuwa layin sabis kuma an bayyana a naúrorin aiki da ƙungiyar ayyuka ke tsarawa a ciki.

## Misali da aka lissafa

Ma'aikatan jinya na musamman na Band 6 suna gudanar da asibitocin tantancewa kafin tiyata. Sarrafa takardu ta atomatik tana dawo da sa'a 1/rana ga kowane ma'aikacin jinya 25; kowace sa'a tana ɗaukar tantancewa 2.

```
Extra assessments = 25 nurses × 2/day × 250 days = 12,500/year
At ~£120 scheme value per pre-op assessment:
  12,500 × £120 = £1.5M/year of care capacity created
```

— ba tare da daukar ma'aikacin jinya ɗaya ko gina ɗaki ɗaya ba. (Samfurin da ake faɗa a ko'ina da wannan rubutu ya fara ambata ya ba da lambar £766,920/shekara ga ƙaramar ƙungiya; tsarin lissafi iri ɗaya ne — lambar tana ƙaruwa da ma'aikatan jinya × zama × kuɗin tarifi.) Tsarin aiki ga COO: tantance kafin tiyata ya daina zama cikas ga jerin ɗakin tiyata — tiyata da ake soke a ranar tana raguwa, kuma amfani da ɗakin tiyata yana ƙaruwa, wanda shi ne inda layin fa'ida *na gaba* ke farawa (duba [inganta albarkatu na ƙasa](../inganta-albarkatu-na-ƙasa/)).

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Sake tsarawa iri ɗaya yana ceton da'awar ƙwazon mai haɓakawa daga lissafin albashi: lokacin injiniya da aka saki, an bayyana shi a matsayin *ƙarfi da aka isar wanda ƙungiyar ba za ta iya biya ba in ba haka ba* — siffofi, ƙaura, aikin amintacce — an daraja a abin da ƙungiyar ke biya don irin wannan ƙarfi a iyaka (farashin ɗan kwangila, ko daidai da daukar ma'aikata da aka jinkirta). Tsarin COO yana kuma koyar da wani abu game da gabatar da aikin dandali: bayyana fa'ida a naúrorin da masu sauraro ke gudanarwa a kai. Shugabannin ayyuka suna tunani a aiki da maƙasudai, ba a sa'o'i na zahiri ba; shugabannin injiniya suna tunani a abubuwan taswirar hanya da ma'aikata, ba a mintuna da aka ceta ba.

## Tarkuna

- **Da'awar ƙarfi ba tare da buƙata ba**: ramukan tantancewa 12,500 na ƙari suna da muhimmanci ne kawai idan bututun tiyata ya cika su — duba ƙuntatawar ƙasa.
- **Darajar tarifi ba tare da hanyar biya ba**: ƙarƙashin biyan gauraye, ƙarin aiki bazai kawo ƙarin kuɗin shiga ba; darajar na iya zama rage jerin jira maimakon haka (duba [tasirin jerin jira](../tasirin-jerin-jira/)).
- **Gabatar da ƙarfi a matsayin kuɗi** — wannan shi ne babban fa'idar da ba ta sakin kuɗi; yi mata alama (duba [tanadi mai sakin kuɗi da wanda ba ya sakin kuɗi](../tanadi-mai-sakin-kuɗi-da-wanda-ba-ya-sakin-kuɗi/)).
