# Dawowar Jari (ROI)

ROI shi ne rabon riba net zuwa kuɗin da aka zuba. Ma'aunin ne da injiniyanci da kuɗi suka riga suka raba — tattalin arzikin lafiya yana ƙara tarbiyyar da ke sa da'awar ROI ta tsira daga bincike: mahanga da aka bayyana, mai kwatantawa, lokaci, da rukunin fa'idodi.

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

ROI shi ne harshen gama-gari na masu rike da kasafi, kuma lafiyar jama'a ma tana amfani da shi: bitar Masters et al. mai tarihi ta gano **matsakaicin ROI na 14.3:1** ga shiga tsakani na lafiyar jama'a (kowane £1 yana dawo da ~£14 ga babban tattalin arziki da tsarin lafiya) — lamba da ake amfani da ita sosai don muhawarar kashe kuɗi kan rigakafi. Amma wannan 14:1 lamba ce ta *al'umma, na dogon lokaci*; ROI na CFO na asibiti mahangar mai biya ne kuma shekara 1–3. Yawancin faɗace-faɗacen ROI a gaskiya faɗace-faɗacen mahanga ne da ba a bayyana ba.

## Lissafin

```
ROI = (Benefits − Costs) / Costs      (often × 100%)

Payback period = Costs / annual net benefit
```

Da'awar ROI ba cikakkiya ba ce ba tare da bayyana abubuwa huɗu ba:

1. **Mahanga** — fa'idodin wane ake ƙidaya? (duba [mahangar bincike](../mahangar-bincike/))
2. **Mai kwatantawa** — a kan wane madadi? (duba [farashin damar da aka bari](../farashin-damar-da-aka-bari/))
3. **Lokaci** — a tsawon lokaci nawa, kuma [an rage daraja](../rage-daraja-da-fifikon-lokaci/)?
4. **Rukunin fa'ida** — mai sakin kuɗi, ƙarfi, ko na inganci? (duba [tanadi mai sakin kuɗi da wanda ba ya sakin kuɗi](../tanadi-mai-sakin-kuɗi-da-wanda-ba-ya-sakin-kuɗi/))

## Misali da aka lissafa

Tsarin e-rostering, kuɗi £500,000 a cikin shekaru 3.

```
Cash-releasing:  agency shift reduction        £450,000
Capacity:        ward-manager admin time freed £600,000 (valued, not banked)
Qualitative:     staff satisfaction, safety    not monetized

Strict financial ROI  = (450,000 − 500,000)/500,000 = −10%
Economic ROI          = (1,050,000 − 500,000)/500,000 = +110%
```

Dukan lambobi biyu gaskiya ne. Mai siyarwa da ke faɗin "ROI +110%" ga CFO da zai iya ajiye £450k kawai zai rasa amana; bayyana duka, an yi wa alama, yana samun ta. Rarrabuwa ɗaya tana kare mai goyon bayan cikin gida lokacin da kuɗi ke binciken fa'idodi bayan shekaru biyu.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Kowace shawarar kayan aiki tana da zane na ROI; kusan babu wanda ke bayyana sigogi huɗun. Mafi yawan gazawa ita ce haɗa rukunai: ribar ƙarfi (mintunan mai haɓakawa) da aka gabatar a matsayin dawowar kuɗi. Tsara ROI na AI/dandali kamar misalin sama — layin kuɗi, layin ƙarfi, layin inganci — sannan ƙara [binciken jin canji](../binciken-jin-canji/) a kan lambobi masu laushi. Don duba gaskiyar P&L ta ROI na AI musamman, duba [dawowar jari ta AI](../dawowar-jari-ta-ai/).

## Tarkuna

- **Wanke mahanga**: fa'idodin al'umma na shekaru goma da aka faɗa wa mai riƙe kasafi da lokaci na wata 12.
- **Jimilla maimakon net**: "yana dawo da £3M" a kan kashe £2M ROI ne 50%, ba 300% ba.
- **Ƙara rabo zuwa mafi girma**: ƙananan masu rabawa suna samar da ROI masu ban mamaki ga ƙananan jari; jera fayiloli bisa NPV ko [ribar kuɗi net](../ribar-kuɗi-net/), yi amfani da ROI a matsayin tacewa.
- **Babu binciken fa'idodi**: ROI da aka hasashe ba tare da bin diddigin [gane fa'idodi](../gane-fa-idodi/) alƙawari ne, ba sakamako ba.
