# Inganta Albarkatu na Ƙasa

Ceton sa'a ɗaya ga ƙwararren ma'aikaci — likitan gida, babban likita mai rajista, ƙwararre — yakan hana jinkirin ƙugiya ga dukan ƙungiyar ma'aikata masu fannoni da yawa (MDT) ta ma'aikatan jinya, magatakarda, da masu magani da ke jiran sa hannun asibiti. Darajar buɗe ƙugiyar ita ce fitarwar duk wanda ke ƙasa da ita.

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

Kula da lafiya yana gudana a kan sarƙoƙin izini: sallama tana jiran sa hannun ƙwararre, tsare-tsaren magani suna jiran bitar MDT, turawa suna jiran rarrabewa. Idan aikin da ke ƙofa ya jinkirta, kuɗin ba sa'ar mutum ɗaya ba ne — lokaci ne maras aiki ko an toshe a duk ayyukan da suka dogara, tare da lokacin majiyyaci a cikin rashin tabbas (ƙarin [kwanakin gado](../kwanakin-gado-da-aka-ceta/), tsawon [jiran RTT](../daga-turawa-zuwa-magani/)). Wannan ka'idar ƙuntatawa ce da aka shafa wa hanyoyin asibiti: sa'a da aka ceta *a ƙuntatawar* tana da darajar fitarwar ƙari ta dukan tsarin; sa'a da aka ceta a wani wuri tana da daraja ƙasa da haka.

## Lissafin

```
Value of unblocking = Σ over downstream roles (blocked hours released × unit cost)
                    + pathway throughput gain × value per pathway completion

Contrast: value of the same hour saved at a non-gating role ≈ that role's
capacity value alone (see practitioner-time.md).
```

A gano ƙuntatawar ta gwaji: a ina aiki ke yin jerin gwano mafi tsawo? Akwatin saƙon wane ne jinkiri ke komawa gare shi?

## Misali da aka lissafa

Sallamar ɗaki tana buƙatar bitar ƙwararre kowace safiya. Ƙwararren yana kashe mintuna 90/rana yana tara bayanai da aka warwatse a tsarin; bita tana ƙarewa da ƙarfe 14:00, kuma sallama 6/rana suna kammalawa a makare ga ranar — kowanne yana kashe ranar gado da za a iya kauce wa.

Allon taƙaitaccen sallama (sakamakon bincike, magunguna, tutoci a gani ɗaya) yana rage tarawa zuwa mintuna 20; bita tana ƙarewa da ƙarfe 11:30:

```
Bed days avoided     = 4 of the 6 late discharges × 365 ≈ 1,460 bed days/year
Downstream unblocking: 2 discharge coordinators + pharmacy + transport
                       previously idle-then-crunched each afternoon —
                       ~3 staff-hours/day of blocked time released ≈ 1,100 hrs/yr
```

Mintuna 70 na ƙwararren da kansa su ne *mafi ƙanƙantar* ɓangaren darajar — manufar wannan ma'aunin. A ƙimanta kwanakin gado bisa hanya (duba [kwanakin gado da aka ceta](../kwanakin-gado-da-aka-ceta/)) da sa'o'in ma'aikata a matsayin ƙarfi.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Wannan shi ne bitar lamba, sa hannun gine-gine, da akwatin saƙon injiniya babba. Idan injiniyoyi biyar suna jiran rana ɗaya ga mutum ɗaya da zai iya amincewa da ƙira, kuɗin ranakun injiniya biyar ne tare da rana ɗaya ta [kuɗin jinkiri](../kuɗin-jinkiri/) a kan aikin kansa — ba sa'ar mai dubawa ɗaya ba. Kayan da ke matsa aikin ƙofar (mafi kyawun mahallin bita, duba-kafin-lokaci ta atomatik, allon da ke tara abin da mai amincewa ke buƙata) yana sayen fitarwar tsarin, ba sauƙin mutum ba. A auna lokacin ɗauka/jira a ƙuntatawar (duba [ma'aunan gudana](../ma-aunan-gudana/)) — shi ne takwaran manhaja na dutsen sallamar 14:00.

## Tarkuna

- **Inganta wanda ba ƙuntatawa ba**: kayan aiki masu kyau ga aikin da babu abin da ke jerin gwano a bayansa yana samar da darajar tsarin kusa da sifili.
- **Ƙaurar ƙuntatawa**: a buɗe ƙwararren kuma ƙuntatawar tana motsawa (zuwa magani, zuwa sufuri) — a yi samfurin ƙuntatawa *ta gaba* kafin a yi da'awar cikakkiyar ribar fitarwa.
- **Ƙidaya sa'o'in ƙasa a matsayin kuɗi**: sakin lokacin da aka toshe ƙarfi ne, ƙarƙashin [gwajin sake turawa](../tanadi-mai-sakin-kuɗi-da-wanda-ba-ya-sakin-kuɗi/) na yau da kullum.
