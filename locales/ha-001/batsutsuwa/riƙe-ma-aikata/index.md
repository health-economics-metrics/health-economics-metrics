# Riƙe Ma'aikata

Tattalin arzikin riƙe ma'aikata yana ƙididdige abin da sauyawar ma'aikata ke kashewa tsarin lafiya — daukar ma'aikata, shigar da su, cike gurbi — don haka darajar manhajar da ke rage gajiyar aikin gudanarwa. Gajiya daga ayyukan bayanan gudanarwa masu maimaitawa babbar hanyar tuƙa sauyawar ma'aikata da rashin zuwa aiki saboda rashin lafiya ce a NHS.

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

Idan likita ya tafi, asibiti tana biyan sau uku: don ɗaukar wanda zai maye gurbinsa (talla, kuɗin hukuma, hira), don shigar da shi (watanni na ƙarancin ƙwazo, sa ido), da kuma don cike gurbin a lokacin — yawanci da ma'aikatan hukuma ko na wucin gadi a 2–3× adadin Agenda for Change na dindindin (duba [kuɗin fitar da aiki waje da za a iya kauce wa](../kuɗin-fitar-da-aiki-waje-da-za-a-iya-kauce-wa/) da [tanadin kuɗi mai tsauri mai sakin kuɗi](../tanadin-kuɗi-mai-tsauri-mai-sakin-kuɗi/)). Domin kuɗin sauyawa kuɗi ne na gaske, ingantuwar riƙewa suna cikin ƙananan fa'idodin ma'aikata da daraktan kuɗi zai iya ajiyewa. Matsalar gudanarwa tana cikin manyan abubuwan da ake ambata na gajiyar asibiti a kai a kai, wanda ke mai da ita kuɗi da manhaja za ta iya magancewa.

## Lissafin

```
Cost per leaver = recruitment cost + onboarding/productivity-ramp cost
                + vacancy cover premium × vacancy duration

Annual turnover cost = headcount × turnover rate × cost per leaver

Value of software  = headcount × Δturnover rate × cost per leaver
                   + sickness-absence reduction × cover cost/day
```

Sarkar dalili tana da hanyoyin haɗi biyu da aka kiyasta — manhaja → gajiya/gogayya, da gajiya → sauyawa — don haka tabbatar da duka biyu da shaida (binciken ma'aikata kafin/bayan; alaƙar gajiya-ƙarewa da aka buga) kuma ka riƙe Δ da aka yi da'awa matsakaici.

## Misali da aka lissafa

Asibiti tana ɗaukar ma'aikatan jinya 1,200; sauyawa 11%/shekara. Kuɗi ga kowane wanda ya tafi:

```
Recruitment ≈ £4,500;  onboarding/ramp ≈ £6,000
Vacancy cover: 4 months × 0.6 WTE covered by agency premium ≈ £8,000
Total ≈ £18,500 per leaver
Baseline turnover cost = 1,200 × 0.11 × 18,500 ≈ £2.44M/year
```

Manhajar rage nauyin takardu (tantancewa da aka cika ta atomatik, shiga ɗaya, dictation) tana iya motsa sauyawa maki 1:

```
Value = 1,200 × 0.01 × 18,500 = £222,000/year cash-relevant
```

Da'awar maki 1 da maki na gogayya na binciken ma'aikata ke goyon baya tana da sahihanci; da'awar maki 4 ba ta da. Yi [guguwa](../binciken-jin-canji/) a kan Δturnover: tana rinjayar duk sauran abubuwa a cikin samfurin.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Lissafin riƙe injiniya iri ɗaya ne kuma ba a rubuta shi sosai ba: maye gurbin babban injiniya yana kashe watanni 6–12 na albashi mai kaya (daukar ma'aikata, ramp, mahallin da aka rasa), don haka ƙungiyar mutane 200 a ƙarewar 15% tana ƙone miliyoyi kowace shekara a sauyawa. Jarin ƙwarewar mai haɓakawa ([SPACE da DevEx](../space-da-devex/)) kwatankwacin kai tsaye ne na sauƙaƙa nauyin takardu ga ma'aikatan jinya — kuma ya kamata a hujjar da shi iri ɗaya: maki na gogayya da aka auna, tasiri mai matsakaici da aka yi da'awa kan ƙarewa, kuɗi ga kowane wanda ya tafi daga bayanan kuɗinka. Tarbiyyar tattalin arzikin lafiya da za a kwafa ita ce *ƙididdige kuɗin wanda ya tafi da gaskiya* maimakon jayayya ko mutane "da gaske" suna tafiya saboda kayan aiki.

## Tarkuna

- **Danganta duk motsin sauyawa ga shiga tsakaninka** — kasuwannin ƙwadago suna motsa sauyawa fiye da manhaja; yi amfani da ƙungiyoyin sarrafawa ko aƙalla daidaita yanayin fage.
- **Ƙirga sau biyu**: tanadin riƙewa da tanadin kashe kuɗin hukuma suna haɗuwa (cike gurbi *shi ne* kashe kuɗin hukuma); daidaita layukan.
- **Watsi da jinkiri**: ƙarewa da gajiya ke tuƙawa tana amsa canje-canjen gogayya a cikin shekaru 1–2, ba kwata na gaba ba.
