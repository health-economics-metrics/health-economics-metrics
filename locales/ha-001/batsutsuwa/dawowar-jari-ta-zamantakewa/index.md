# Dawowar Jari ta Zamantakewa (SROI)

SROI yana faɗaɗa [ROI](../dawowar-jari/) zuwa sakamakon da kasuwanni ba sa yi musu farashi — jin daɗi, dangantakar zamantakewa, tasirin muhalli — ta hanyar mayar da su kuɗi da wakilan kuɗi, ga *dukan* masu ruwa da tsaki da abin ya shafa.

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

Yawancin abin da shiga tsakani na lafiya da al'umma ke samarwa ba ya taɓa layin kasafi: rage kaɗaici, sauƙin masu kulawa, ribar aiki, mutunci. SROI, wanda ƙa'idodi bakwai na Social Value International ke jagoranta (shigar da masu ruwa da tsaki, daraja abin da ke da muhimmanci, kada ka wuce gona da iri a da'awa, a bayyane, tabbatarwa…), yana samar da kalamai kamar "£3.20 na darajar zamantakewa ga kowane £1 da aka zuba." Buƙatun darajar zamantakewa na sayayyar jama'a ta Burtaniya suna mai da shaidar irin SROI muhimmiya a kasuwanci: tayin kwangilar jama'a (har da NHS) suna samun maki saboda darajar zamantakewa da aka nuna.

## Lissafin

```
SROI ratio = PV(monetized social outcomes) / PV(investment)

For each outcome:
  value = quantity × financial proxy × attribution × (1 − deadweight) × (1 − displacement)

deadweight   = would have happened anyway
attribution  = share caused by others
displacement = benefit moved from elsewhere rather than created
drop-off     = decay of the outcome over years
```

Abubuwan daidaitawa su ne amincin hanyar: ba tare da su ba, SROI ƙagaggen labari ne da alamar kuɗi.

## Misali da aka lissafa

Manhajar abokantaka da ke haɗa tsofaffi keɓaɓɓu da masu sa kai; kuɗin shirin £200,000/shekara; ma'aurata masu aiki 1,500.

```
Outcome: reduced loneliness for 1,500 people
  proxy: wellbeing valuation of "relief from loneliness" ≈ £1,800/person/yr
  deadweight 25% (some would have found connection anyway)
  attribution 80% (some credit to other services)

Value = 1,500 × 1,800 × 0.80 × 0.75 = £1,620,000

Outcome: reduced GP visits, 1,500 × 1.2 visits × £42 = £75,600 (payer-real)

SROI = (1,620,000 + 75,600) / 200,000 ≈ 8.5 : 1
```

Lura rabon ya kasance 96% jin daɗi da aka daraja ta wakili da 4% kuɗi na gaske. Wannan SROI ne mai ƙa'ida — amma dole a gabatar da shi a matsayin darajar zamantakewa, kada a bar shi ya nuna £1.7M ana iya ajiyewa.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

SROI shi ne tsarin gaskiya don aikin injiniya da masu cin gajiyarsa ke wajen ƙungiyar da ke biya: kula da buɗaɗɗen tushe, inganta samun dama, aikin dandali da wasu ƙungiyoyi ke amfani da shi, jarin al'ummar masu haɓakawa. Tsarin da ake canjawa: gano duk masu ruwa da tsaki, mayar da su kuɗi da wakilai da aka bayyana, da amfani da rangwamen deadweight/attribution (shin wannan gyaran OSS zai faru ko ta yaya? nawa na ribar aikinka ne da na muhallin?). Tarbiyyar *rangwame ga da'awar tasirinka* ita ce ke raba SROI da lambar talla.

## Tarkuna

- **Zaɓen wakili**: zaɓar mafi karimcin darajar jin daɗi da ake da shi.
- **Tsallake deadweight/attribution** — mafi yawan ƙarin ƙima, sau da yawa yana ninka rabon.
- **Kwatanta rabo a faɗin bincike**: rabon SROI yana kula da hanya; kwatanta a cikin tsari mai daidaito kawai.
- **Gabatar da darajar zamantakewa a matsayin tanadi da za a iya ajiyewa** ga mai riƙe kasafi.
