# Hanyar Gaggawa ta DiGA ta Jamus

DiGA (Digitale Gesundheitsanwendungen) hanyar doka ce ta Jamus ta "manhajoji da takardar magani" — tsarin ƙasa na farko a duniya inda likitoci ke rubuta manhajojin lafiya da aka amince da su kuma inshorar doka dole ta mayar da kuɗinsu. Shi ne babban gwajin kai tsaye na biyan kuɗin magungunan dijital a girman ƙasa.

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

DiGA ta amsa tambayar da kowane kamfanin lafiyar dijital ke yi — "wa zai biya da gaske?" — da doka (DVG, 2019). Ƙirar abin mamaki ce:

- **Yanke shawara cikin sauri**: BfArM (mai ƙa'ida) dole ta yanke shawara cikin watanni 3.
- **Jerin wucin gadi**: manhajoji za su iya shiga jeri na watanni 12 *alhali suna ci gaba da samar da shaida* — suna samun kuɗin shiga yayin binciken ainihinsu.
- **Wa'adin shaida**: a tabbatar da "tasirin kula da lafiya mai kyau" (fa'idar likita, ko ingantaccen tsari/hanya mai alaƙa da majiyyaci) ta binciken kwatantawa — yawanci RCT — ko a cire daga jerin. Kusan rabin shigarwar wucin gadi sun kasa komawa na dindindin.
- **Farashi**: mai ƙera yana saita farashin shekara ta 1 da yardar kansa; sannan a tattauna da ƙungiyar masu inshora. Matsakaicin farashin farko na watanni 3 kusan €500; abubuwan farashi bisa aiki suna zuwa daga 2026.

Duba gaskiyar kasuwa (bincike har ƙarshen 2024): ~manhajoji 68 a jeri, >takardun magani miliyan 1 gaba ɗaya, ~81% na takardun an kunna su, ~€234M kashe kuɗin masu inshora gaba ɗaya — kasuwa ta gaske, amma ƙarama idan aka kwatanta da hayaniyar, kuma bin ka'ida bayan kunnawa har yanzu shi ne rauni.

## Lissafin

Samfurin kasuwanci da kowane wanda ya kafa DiGA ke gudanarwa:

```
Revenue = prescriptions × activation rate × price per prescription period
Evidence cost = pivotal RCT (typically €1M–3M) within the 12-month window
Expected value = P(evidence succeeds) × steady-state revenue − evidence cost

With ~50% conversion failure, P must be honestly assessed — half the field
spends the RCT money and loses the listing.
```

## Misali da aka lissafa

Manhajar sarrafa baƙin ciki tana shiga jerin wucin gadi a €450/kwata:

```
Year 1: 20,000 prescriptions × 81% activation × €450 ≈ €7.3M revenue
RCT cost: €2M, running concurrently
Outcome A (evidence positive): permanent listing, negotiated price ~€380,
  steady state 60,000 scripts/yr ≈ €18.5M/yr
Outcome B (evidence fails): delisted at month 12; revenue stops.
```

Shekarar wucin gadi tana ba da kuɗin samar da shaida — babbar sabuwar dabarar hanyar. A kwatanta da tsarin na gargajiya (shaida da farko, kuɗin shiga bayan shekaru), wanda ke yunwatar da daidai samfuran da DiGA ke so su wanzu.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Tsarin DiGA — **ɗauka na wucin gadi tare da ma'aunin nasara da aka yi wa rajista tukuna da ƙarewa ta atomatik** — ana iya kwafe shi kai tsaye don mulkin kayan injiniya: a aika kayan ga masu amfani na samarwa na watanni 12, a yi wa ma'auni rajista tukuna (lokacin da aka auna da aka ceta, rage abubuwan da suka faru), a bar shi ya ƙare ta atomatik sai dai idan shaida ta zo. Yana magance rikitarwar gwaji (kayan da ke buƙatar girma don tabbatar da daraja ba sa samun girma) ba tare da ba da dindindin ga fasahar da ba a tabbatar ba. Bayanan kunnawa 81%/bin ka'ida maras yawa kuma suna ɗauke da darasi na samfur: takardar magani (ko umarnin shugaba) tana samar da shigarwa; ingancin samfur kaɗai ke samar da ci gaba da amfani — duba [bin ka'ida da dorewa](../bin-ka-ida-da-dorewa/).

## Tarkuna

- **Ɗaukar jeri a matsayin layin ƙarshe** — takardun magani suna buƙatar amanar mai rubutawa; yawancin DiGA da ke jeri suna ganin ƙaramar yawa.
- **Rashin isasshen ƙarfin binciken ainihi** don tanadar kuɗi a shekarar kuɗin shiga — tattalin arzikin ƙarya da ke bayyana yawancin kashi 50% na gazawa.
- **Dasa samfurin ba tare da mai biya ba**: DiGA tana aiki saboda mayar da kuɗi na doka ne; kwafi ba tare da biyan da aka wajabta ba shiri ne na gwaji kawai.
