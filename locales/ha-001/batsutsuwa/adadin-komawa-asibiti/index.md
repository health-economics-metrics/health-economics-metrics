# Adadin Komawa Asibiti

Adadin komawa asibiti na kwanaki 30 shi ne kashi na marasa lafiya da aka sallama waɗanda suke dawowa a gaggauce cikin kwanaki 30. Shi ne ma'aunin *ingancin sallama* na ƙa'ida na tsarin lafiya — kuma yana ɗauke da hukunce-hukuncen kuɗi kai tsaye.

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

Komawa yana nufin sallamar farko ba ta ɗore ba: sallama da wuri, gazawar mika magani, rashin bibiya, ko rashin tallafin zamantakewa. Masu biya suna hukunta shi a bayyane — Shirin Rage Komawa Asibiti na Amurka yana yanke har 3% na biyan Medicare na asibiti; NHS a tarihi ba ta biya komawar gaggawa ta kwanaki 30 da za a iya kauce wa ba. Don haka kauce wa komawa yana ɗaya daga cikin ƙananan rukunonin fa'ida da suke da muhimmanci *kai tsaye* ga kuɗin mai bayarwa, ba ƙarfi kawai ba.

## Lissafin

```
Readmission rate = emergency readmissions within 30 days / index discharges × 100

Risk-standardized comparisons adjust for case mix; penalty programmes
compare observed vs expected for like hospitals.

Value of avoidance = avoided readmissions × (cost per readmission spell
                     + penalty exposure per readmission)
```

## Misali da aka lissafa

Manhajar tallafin sallama ta ciwon zuciya (bin diddigin alamomi, faɗakarwar nauyi, tunatarwar magani, ɗaga zuwa ma'aikaciyar jinya): sallama 2,000/shekara, adadin komawa na tushe 18%, gwaji ya nuna 14% tare da manhajar.

```
Avoided readmissions = 2,000 × (0.18 − 0.14) = 80/year
Cost per readmission spell ≈ £3,500 → £280,000/year avoided treatment cost
Plus penalty/non-payment exposure on those spells.
App cost: 2,000 × £60 = £120,000/year

Net ≈ +£160,000/year, before any QALY claim for avoided deterioration.
```

Lambar da za a kare ita ce tasirin maki 4: dole ta fito daga kwatance mai sarrafawa, domin adadin komawa yana canzawa da gauraya na marasa lafiya da kakar.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Komawa shi ne **adadin gazawar canji** na tsarin lafiya (duba [ma'aunan DORA](../ma-aunan-dora/)): aikin da "aka fitar" amma ya dawo cikin kwanaki 30. Kamanceceniyar tana da zurfi — tikiti da aka sake buɗewa da abubuwan koma baya suna nuna rashin "ingancin sallama" (rauni a tabbatarwa, rufewa da wuri, rashin takardun mika aiki); lissafin salon hukunci (ƙungiyar da ke gyarawa tana biya, ba mai karɓa ba) yana canza hali; kuma fagage biyu sun koyi darasi ɗaya, cewa tura ƙarfin isarwa (sallama da sauri, fitarwa da sauri) ba tare da zuba jari a mika aiki ba kawai yana mayar da jerin gwano masu gani zuwa sake aiki marar gani. "Adadin sake buɗewa na kwanaki 30" ya kamata ya kasance a dashboard na kowace ƙungiya da ke murnar lokacin zagaye.

## Tarkuna

- **Yin wasa da lambobi ta sake sanya suna**: komawa da aka yi wa lamba a matsayin zaman lura ko sabbin yanayi; duba ma'anar.
- **Duk dalili da dalili mai alaƙa**: duk-dalili na kwanaki 30 yana ƙunshe da abubuwa marasa alaƙa na gaske; hukunce-hukunce yawanci suna amfani da duk-dalili daidai domin "mai alaƙa" ana iya yin ƙwanƙwasa da shi.
- **Makantar gauraya na marasa lafiya**: asibitin da ke yi wa al'ummomi marasa lafiya da talauci hidima yana da ƙarin komawa saboda dalilan da babu manhajar da ke gyarawa — daidaita haɗari kafin kwatantawa.
