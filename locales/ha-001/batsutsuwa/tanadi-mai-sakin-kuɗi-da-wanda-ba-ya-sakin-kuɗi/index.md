# Tanadi Mai Sakin Kuɗi da Wanda Ba Ya Sakin Kuɗi

Tanadi mai sakin kuɗi yana rage kashe kuɗi na zahiri — layin kasafin kuɗi yana ƙanƙancewa. Tanadi mara sakin kuɗi yana 'yantar da lokaci ko ƙarfi wanda ake *sake amfani da shi* maimakon a ajiye shi a banki. Daraktocin kuɗi na tsarin lafiya suna ɗaukar waɗannan a matsayin jinsuna daban-daban, kuma ya kamata ku ma haka.

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

Wannan shi ne mafi kaifin gwajin gaskiya da ake yi wa kowace shari'ar kasuwanci ta dijital a hidimar lafiya ta ƙasa. Tsarin fa'ida na NHS yana rarraba kowace fa'ida da aka yi da'awa a fili a matsayin mai sakin kuɗi, mara sakin kuɗi, ko na inganci. Yawancin "tanadi" na lafiyar dijital — mintunan likita da aka ceta ga kowane majiyyaci, saurin rubuta bayanai — ba sa sakin kuɗi: masu daraja, amma ba sa rage gibi. CFO na asusun asibiti da ke fuskantar gibin kuɗi zai iya kashe kuɗi kawai. Duba kuma [tanadin kuɗi mai tsauri mai sakin kuɗi](../tanadin-kuɗi-mai-tsauri-mai-sakin-kuɗi/).

## Lissafin

```
Cash-releasing saving   = budget line before − budget line after
                          (must be extractable: a cancelled contract, closed ward,
                           reduced agency spend, avoided purchase)

Non-cash-releasing value = time released × unit cost of that time
                          (valued at opportunity cost; the money is NOT extractable)
```

Abu ɗaya na zahiri (sa'a da aka ceta) yana faɗuwa a rukuni ɗaya ko wani bisa abin da ke biyo baya:

```
hour saved → overtime/agency shift cancelled        → cash-releasing
hour saved → clinician sees one more waiting patient → non-cash-releasing (capacity)
hour saved → absorbed into slack, nothing changes    → no benefit at all
```

## Misali da aka lissafa

Manhaja tana ceton kowace ma'aikaciyar jinya cikin 100 mintuna 30 a kowace aiki. Wannan 100 × 0.5 × zaman 5/mako × makonni 46 ≈ sa'o'i 11,500/shekara. A kuɗin ma'aikaci na Band 5 na ~£25/sa'a, kanun labarai mai jan hankali shi ne £287,500/shekara.

Rabon gaskiya:

- 20% na lokacin yana fāɗuwa inda ɗakuna a yanzu ke biyan ƙarin kuɗin bank/hukuma don rufe wuce gona da iri na rubuta bayanai: sa'o'i 2,300 × £35 ƙimar hukuma = **£80,500 mai sakin kuɗi** (ba a yi rajistar aiki da gaske ba).
- 60% an mayar da shi zuwa kulawa kai tsaye ga marasa lafiya: sa'o'i 6,900 × £25 = **£172,500 ƙarfi mara sakin kuɗi** — daraja ta gaske, ana bayar da rahoto daban, ba a taɓa kiran ta "tanadi" ba.
- 20% yana ɓacewa cikin hutu da katsewa: **£0**. Da'awar ta almara ce.

Shari'ar kasuwanci da ke gabatar da £80.5k kuɗi + £172.5k ƙarfi amintacciya ce. Wadda ke gabatar da "tanadi" £287.5k tana samun ƙi daga ma'aikacin lissafi na farko da ya karanta ta.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

Dabarar iri ɗaya tana mulkin ROI na mataimakin rubuta lamba na AI: "mintuna 30 ga kowane mai haɓakawa a kowace rana" ƙarfi ne mara sakin kuɗi sai dai idan yawan ma'aikata, kuɗin ɗan kwangila, ko kuɗin gajimare ya ragu da gaske. A bayar da rahoton rukunai daban:

- Mai sakin kuɗi: kwangilolin ɗan kwangila da aka soke, lasisin kayan aiki da aka kashe, rage kashe kuɗin gajimare.
- Ƙarfi: fasaloli da aka aika da wuri (daraja ta [kuɗin jinkiri](../kuɗin-jinkiri/)), jerin aiki da aka rage.
- Komai: mintuna da aka ceta waɗanda suke rarrabuwa zuwa sauya mahallin aiki.

A kuma bi diddigin *inda lokacin da aka 'yantar ya tafi a zahiri* — gane fa'idodi ([benefits-realization.md](../gane-fa-idodi/)) yana wanzuwa saboda ribar ƙarfi da aka yi da'awa tana ƙafewa sau da yawa idan an duba.

## Tarkuna

- **Ninka mintuna da albashi a kira shi tanadi** — babban zunubin.
- **Ƙimanta lokacin da aka 'yantar da matsakaicin kuɗin da aka ɗora** alhali amfanin lokacin na ƙari ba shi da daraja — duba [kuɗin ƙari da matsakaicin kuɗi](../kuɗin-ƙari-da-matsakaicin-kuɗi/).
- **Ƙidaya sa'a ɗaya sau biyu**: a matsayin kuɗi (an kauce wa aiki) da ƙarfi (an ga ƙarin marasa lafiya).
