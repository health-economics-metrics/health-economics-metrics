# Binciken Tasirin Kuɗi (CEA)

CEA tana kwatanta kuɗin shiga tsakani dabam-dabam da sakamako ɗaya da aka auna a **naúrorin halitta** — shekarun rayuwa, lamuran da aka gano, shigar asibiti da aka kauce wa, mmHg na rage hawan jini. Fitarwarta kuɗi ne ga kowace naúrar sakamako.

## Dalilin muhimmancinsa

CEA ita ce kwatancen aiki lokacin da duk zaɓuɓɓuka ke nufin sakamako ɗaya. Tana amsa "wanne daga cikin waɗannan hanyoyin cimma X ne mafi kyawun amfani da kuɗi?" — amma *ba* "ko X ya cancanci cimmawa kwata-kwata?" (wannan yana buƙatar [binciken riba da kuɗi](../binciken-riba-da-kuɗi/)) kuma *ba* "yaya X ya kwatanta da fifiko marasa alaƙa?" (wannan yana buƙatar [binciken amfanin kuɗi](../binciken-amfanin-kuɗi/) da sakamako na gama-gari kamar QALY).

## Lissafin

Ƙididdigar kwatancen ita ce [ICER](../rabon-ƙarin-tasirin-kuɗi/) a naúrorin halitta:

```
ICER = (Cost_A − Cost_B) / (Effect_A − Effect_B)
     = £ per additional case detected / admission avoided / etc.
```

Hanya: a ayyana naúrar sakamako; a ƙayyade farashin kowane zaɓi daga [mahanga](../mahangar-bincike/) ɗaya a tsawon [lokaci](../tsawon-lokacin-bincike/) ɗaya; a kawar da zaɓuɓɓukan da aka mamaye ([iyakar inganci](../mamaya-da-iyakar-inganci/)); a lissafa rabon ƙari a kan iyakar.

## Misali da aka lissafa

Hanyoyi uku na gano atrial fibrillation da ba a gano ba a al'umma 100,000:

```
Option                    Cost        Cases found
Opportunistic pulse checks £150,000       300
Pharmacy screening events  £400,000       520
Wearable-based screening   £900,000       610

ICER pharmacy vs pulse:  (400k−150k)/(520−300) = £1,136 per additional case
ICER wearable vs pharmacy:(900k−400k)/(610−520) = £5,556 per additional case
```

Ko £5,556 ga kowane ƙarin lamari "ya cancanta" yana dogara ne a kan darajar lamarin da aka samo (rigakafin bugun jini na ƙasa) — CEA tana jera zaɓuɓɓuka amma shawarar ɗauka tana buƙatar wannan ƙimanta na waje. A lura cewa *matsakaicin* kuɗin kowane lamari na zaɓin na'urar sawa (900k/610 = £1,475) yana da kyau; £5,556 na *ƙari* shi ne lambar gaskiya don shawarar faɗaɗawa.

## Alaƙa da injiniyancin manhaja

CEA ita ce samfurin da ya dace a duk lokacin da zaɓuɓɓuka ke raba sakamako ɗaya: kuɗi ga kowane gwaji maras tsayayye da aka kawar a hanyoyin gyara uku; kuɗi ga kowane abin da ya faru da aka kauce wa a masu sayar da sa ido; kuɗi ga kowane aikawa mai nasara a gine-ginen CI. Horon da take tilastawa — naúrar sakamako ɗaya da aka bayyana, rabo na ƙari (ba matsakaici ba), a kawar da zaɓuɓɓukan da aka mamaye tukuna — yana kashe yawancin munanan kwatancen masu sayarwa kafin tattaunawar farashi ta fara.

## Tarkuna

- **Kwatanta zaɓuɓɓuka masu sakamako daban-daban** ("lamuran da aka samo" da "gamsuwa") a CEA ɗaya — wannan yana buƙatar [binciken sakamakon kuɗi](../binciken-sakamakon-kuɗi/) ko sakamako na gama-gari.
- **Rabon tasirin kuɗi na matsakaici** da aka gabatar inda ake buƙatar na ƙari (misalin na'urar sawa na sama).
- **Naúrorin sakamako da aka zaɓa don yabo**: "faɗakarwa da aka samar" fitarwa ce, ba sakamako ba; a nace a kan naúrorin da ke ɗauke da daraja.
